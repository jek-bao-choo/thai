
Page 10 of _**phuut thai 2.pdf**_ (Lesson 1, Part B: Vocabulary / คำศัพท์) teaches **daily activity vocabulary paired with time-matching exercises**. The page presents six common daily activity phrases in a vocabulary box and asks students to listen to audio descriptions of students' daily schedules and match each activity to digital clock times (such as 08:00, 09:30, 11:00, 13:00, 14:00, 15:30, 20:00, 00:00, 02:00).

Below is the detailed word breakdown and conversational practice for each of the six target vocabulary phrases taught on page 10:

---

### 1. **ไปรับเพื่อนที่บ้าน** (_pay ráp phûan thîi báan_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Go + receive/pick up + friend + at + home.
2. **Meaning**: To go pick up a friend at their home.
3. **Word Breakdown**:
    - **ไป** (_pay_)
        - _4. Meaning_: Go. Motion verb directed away from the speaker.
        - _5. Usage_: Used constantly when heading to any location.
        - _6. Formality_: Neutral.
        - _7. Daily Example_: **ผมจะไปทำงานครับ** (_phŏm ca pay tham-ŋaan khráp_) – I will go to work.
        - _8. Synonyms_: **ไปยัง** (_pay yaŋ_ – formal/written).
    - **รับ** (_ráp_)
        - _4. Meaning_: Pick up / Receive.
        - _5. Usage_: Used daily when picking someone up in a car or receiving items.
        - _6. Formality_: Neutral.
        - _7. Daily Example_: **มารับผมด้วยนะครับ** (_maa ráp phŏm dûay ná khráp_) – Please come pick me up too.
        - _8. Synonyms_: **ต้อนรับ** (_tɔ̂ɔn-ráp_ – welcome/receive formally).
    - **เพื่อน** (_phûan_)
        - _4. Meaning_: Friend.
        - _5. Usage_: Standard everyday term for a friend, classmate, or peer.
        - _6. Formality_: Neutral.
        - _7. Daily Example_: **เพื่อนผมมาหาที่บ้าน** (_phŏm phûan maa hăa thîi báan_) – My friend came to visit at home.
        - _8. Synonyms_: **สหาย** (_sa-hăai_ – literary/formal, rarely used in daily conversation).
    - **ที่** (_thîi_)
        - _4. Meaning_: At / In (location preposition).
        - _5. Usage_: Standard preposition marking location.
        - _6. Formality_: Neutral.
        - _7. Daily Example_: **เจอกันที่ร้านอาหารครับ** (_cəə kan thîi ráan ?aahăan khráp_) – Meet at the restaurant.
        - _8. Synonyms_: **ณ** (_ná_ – formal/written).
    - **บ้าน** (_báan_)
        - _4. Meaning_: Home / House.
        - _5. Usage_: Everyday noun for home or hometown.
        - _6. Formality_: Neutral.
        - _7. Daily Example_: **ผมอยากกลับบ้านครับ** (_phŏm yàak klàp báan khráp_) – I want to go home.
        - _8. Synonyms_: **ที่พัก** (_thîi-phák_ – residence).
4. **Thai Conversational Version**: **ผมจะไปรับเพื่อนที่บ้านครับ** (_phŏm ca pay ráp phûan thîi báan khráp_) – I am going to pick up my friend at home.

### 2. **ไปซื้ออาหารที่ตลาด** (_pay sÚu ?aahăan thîi talàat_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Go + buy + food + at + market.
2. **Meaning**: To go buy food at the market.
3. **Word Breakdown**:
    - **ซื้อ** (_sÚu_)
        - _4. Meaning_: Buy.
        - _5. Usage_: Core commercial verb used daily in transactions.
        - _6. Formality_: Neutral.
        - _7. Daily Example_: **ผมอยากซื้ออันนี้ครับ** (_phŏm yàak sÚu an níi khráp_) – I want to buy this one.
        - _8. Synonyms_: **จัดซื้อ** (_càt-sÚu_ – formal/corporate purchasing).
    - **อาหาร** (_?aahăan_)
        - _4. Meaning_: Food / Meal.
        - _5. Usage_: Standard noun for meals and food.
        - _6. Formality_: Neutral to Formal.
        - _7. Daily Example_: **อาหารไทยอร่อยมากครับ** (_?aahăan thay ?aròy mâak khráp_) – Thai food is very delicious.
        - _8. Synonyms_: **กับข้าว** (_kàp-khâaw_ – casual dishes eaten with rice), **ของกิน** (_khɔ̌ɔŋ-kin_ – casual food/snacks).
    - **ตลาด** (_talàat_)
        - _4. Meaning_: Market.
        - _5. Usage_: Everyday word for fresh markets or night markets.
        - _6. Formality_: Neutral.
        - _7. Daily Example_: **ผมไปเดินเล่นที่ตลาดครับ** (_phŏm pay dəən len thîi talàat khráp_) – I went to walk around the market.
        - _8. Synonyms_: **ตลาดสด** (_talàat-sòt_ – fresh market).
4. **Thai Conversational Version**: **ผมไปซื้ออาหารที่ตลาดครับ** (_phŏm pay sÚu ?aahăan thîi talàat khráp_) – I go buy food at the market.

### 3. **เอากางบ้านไปส่ง** (_?aw kaanbáan pay sòŋ_)

_(Note: Printed as **เอากางบ้านไปส่ง** in textbook as a typo for **การบ้าน** kaanbáan)_

1. **Literal Translation**: Take/bring + homework + go + submit/deliver.
2. **Meaning**: To take homework to submit (to the teacher).
3. **Word Breakdown**:
    - **เอา** (_?aw_)
        - _4. Meaning_: Take / Bring / Want.
        - _5. Usage_: Very common in informal and neutral daily speech for taking or ordering items.
        - _6. Formality_: Casual to Neutral.
        - _7. Daily Example_: **ผมเอาอันนี้ครับ** (_phŏm ?aw an níi khráp_) – I'll take this one.
        - _8. Synonyms_: **นำ** (_nam_ – formal "bring/lead").
    - **การบ้าน** (_kaanbáan_)
        - _4. Meaning_: Homework.
        - _5. Usage_: Standard academic noun used by students daily.
        - _6. Formality_: Neutral.
        - _7. Daily Example_: **ผมทำการบ้านเสร็จแล้วครับ** (_phŏm tham kaanbáan sèt lɛ́ɛw khráp_) – I finished doing my homework.
        - _8. Synonyms_: **แบบฝึกหัด** (_bɛ̀ɛp-fUuk-hàt_ – exercises/worksheets).
    - **ส่ง** (_sòŋ_)
        - _4. Meaning_: Submit / Send / Deliver.
        - _5. Usage_: Used when delivering mail, dropping off friends, or handing in homework.
        - _6. Formality_: Neutral.
        - _7. Daily Example_: **ผมจะไปส่งงานครับ** (_phŏm ca pay sòŋ ŋaan khráp_) – I am going to submit work.
        - _8. Synonyms_: **ยื่น** (_yUun_ – hand over/submit formally).
4. **Thai Conversational Version**: **ผมจะเอาการบ้านไปส่งอาจารย์ครับ** (_phŏm ca ?aw kaanbáan pay sòŋ ?aacaan khráp_) – I am going to take my homework to submit to the professor.

### 4. **ไปดูหนัง** (_pay duu năŋ_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Go + watch + movie.
2. **Meaning**: To go watch a movie.
3. **Word Breakdown**:
    - **ดู** (_duu_)
        - _4. Meaning_: Watch / Look at.
        - _5. Usage_: Used constantly for watching TV, movies, or looking at something.
        - _6. Formality_: Neutral.
        - _7. Daily Example_: **ดูนี่สิครับ** (_duu níi sì khráp_) – Look at this!
        - _8. Synonyms_: **มอง** (_mɔɔŋ_ – look at), **ชม** (_chom_ – formal view/watch).
    - **หนัง** (_năŋ_)
        - _4. Meaning_: Movie / Film.
        - _5. Usage_: Everyday conversational word for cinema/movies (short for **ภาพยนตร์**).
        - _6. Formality_: Neutral / Conversational.
        - _7. Daily Example_: **หนังเรื่องนี้สนุกมากครับ** (_năŋ rUaŋ níi sanùk mâak khráp_) – This movie is very fun.
        - _8. Synonyms_: **ภาพยนตร์** (_phâap-pha-yon_ – formal/official term for film).
4. **Thai Conversational Version**: **วันเสาร์นี้ผมจะไปดูหนังกับเพื่อนครับ** (_wan săw níi phŏm ca pay duu năŋ kàp phûan khráp_) – This Saturday I am going to watch a movie with my friend.

### 5. **มีเรียน** (_mii rian_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Have + study/class.
2. **Meaning**: To have class / lectures.
3. **Word Breakdown**:
    - **มี** (_mii_)
        - _4. Meaning_: Have / Exist.
        - _5. Usage_: Universal verb for possession or existence.
        - _6. Formality_: Neutral.
        - _7. Daily Example_: **ผมมีเวลาครับ** (_phŏm mii wee-laa khráp_) – I have time.
        - _8. Synonyms_: **ครอบครอง** (_khrɔ̂ɔp-khrɔɔŋ_ – formal).
    - **เรียน** (_rian_)
        - _4. Meaning_: Study / Attend class.
        - _5. Usage_: Daily academic verb used by students and teachers.
        - _6. Formality_: Neutral.
        - _7. Daily Example_: **ผมเรียนภาษาไทยทุกวันครับ** (_phŏm rian phaa-săa thay thúk wan khráp_) – I study Thai every day.
        - _8. Synonyms_: **ศึกษา** (_sUk-săa_ – formal/academic study).
4. **Thai Conversational Version**: **วันนี้ผมมีเรียนตอนเช้าครับ** (_wan níi phŏm mii rian toon cháaw khráp_) – Today I have class in the morning.

### 6. **อ่านหนังสือที่ห้องสมุด** (_?àan naŋsUu thîi hông samùt_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Read + book + at + room + notebook/manuscript (library).
2. **Meaning**: To read books at the library.
3. **Word Breakdown**:
    - **อ่าน** (_?àan_)
        - _4. Meaning_: Read.
        - _5. Usage_: Standard verb for reading books, messages, or news.
        - _6. Formality_: Neutral.
        - _7. Daily Example_: **ผมชอบอ่านหนังสือครับ** (_phŏm chɔ̂ɔp ?àan naŋsUu khráp_) – I like reading books.
        - _8. Synonyms_: **อ่านออกเสียง** (_?àan ?ɔ̀ɔk síaŋ_ – read aloud).
    - **หนังสือ** (_naŋsUu_)
        - _4. Meaning_: Book.
        - _5. Usage_: Standard noun for books and study materials .
        - _6. Formality_: Neutral.
        - _7. Daily Example_: **หนังสือเล่มนี้ดีมากครับ** (_naŋsUu lêm níi dii mâak khráp_) – This book is very good.
        - _8. Synonyms_: **ตำรา** (_tam-raa_ – textbook/formal).
    - **ห้องสมุด** (_hông samùt_)
        - _4. Meaning_: Library.
        - _5. Usage_: Compound from **ห้อง** (_room_) + **สมุด** (_notebook_).
        - _6. Formality_: Neutral.
        - _7. Daily Example_: **ห้องสมุดเงียบมากครับ** (_hông samùt ŋîap mâak khráp_) – The library is very quiet.
        - _8. Synonyms_: **หอสมุด** (_hɔ̌ɔ samùt_ – large/national library, formal).
4. **Thai Conversational Version**: **ผมชอบไปอ่านหนังสือที่ห้องสมุดครับ** (_phŏm chɔ̂ɔp pay ?àan naŋsUu thîi hông samùt khráp_) – I like going to read books at the library.

To read the clock times on **page 10 of _Phuut Thai 2_**, Thai uses a traditional system that divides the 24-hour day into distinct cultural periods (morning, afternoon, late afternoon, night, and overnight) rather than relying on a simple 12-hour AM/PM system.

Here is how to read each of the nine digital clock times shown on page 10:

1. **08:00** (8:00 AM) – **แปดโมงเช้า** (_pɛ̀ɛt mooŋ cháaw_)
2. **09:30** (9:30 AM) – **เก้าโมงครึ่ง** (_kâaw mooŋ khrÛŋ_)
3. **11:00** (11:00 AM) – **สิบเอ็ดโมง** (_sìp ?et mooŋ_)
4. **13:00** (1:00 PM) – **บ่ายโมง** (_bàay mooŋ_)
5. **14:00** (2:00 PM) – **บ่ายสองโมง** (_bàay sɔ̌ɔŋ mooŋ_)
6. **15:30** (3:30 PM) – **บ่ายสามโมงครึ่ง** (_bàay săam mooŋ khrÛŋ_)
7. **20:00** (8:00 PM) – **สองทุ่ม** (_sɔ̌ɔŋ thûm_)
8. **00:00** (12:00 Midnight) – **เที่ยงคืน** (_thîaŋ khUun_)
9. **02:00** (2:00 AM) – **ตีสอง** (_thii sɔ̌ɔŋ_)

---

### Detailed Key Vocabulary Breakdown

#### 1. โมง (_mooŋ_)

1. **Literal Translation**: O'clock / Daytime hour.
2. **Meaning**: Standard classifier used for telling daytime hours between 6:00 AM and 5:59 PM.
3. **Word Breakdown**: **โมง** (_mooŋ_) – Mid tone.
4. **Cultural & Historical Context**: Historically derived from the resonant sound of large gongs (_mooŋ...mooŋ_) struck in village towers in ancient Siam to signal daytime hours.
5. **Modern Usage**: Used constantly in daily life when asking or telling daytime time.
6. **Formality**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **ตอนนี้กี่โมงแล้วครับ** (_toon níi kìi mooŋ lɛ́ɛw khráp_) – What time is it now?
8. **Synonyms**: **นาฬิกา** (_naa-li-kaa_ – formal/official o'clock).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **ตอนนี้แปดโมงเช้าครับ** (_toon níi pɛ̀ɛt mooŋ cháaw khráp_) – It is 8:00 AM now.

---

#### 2. บ่าย (_bàay_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Afternoon / Post-noon.
2. **Meaning**: Time prefix used exclusively for afternoon hours from 1:00 PM through 3:59 PM.
3. **Word Breakdown**: **บ่าย** (_bàay_) – Low tone.
4. **Cultural & Historical Context**: Refers to the sun shifting past its peak position into the afternoon sky.
5. **Modern Usage**: Essential for setting afternoon appointments, classes, or lunch plans.
6. **Formality**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **ผมมีเรียนตอนบ่ายสองโมงครับ** (_phŏm mii rian toon bàay sɔ̌ɔŋ mooŋ khráp_) – I have class at 2:00 PM.
8. **Synonyms**: **ช่วงบ่าย** (_chûaŋ bàay_ – afternoon period).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **เจอกันตอนบ่ายโมงครับ** (_cəə kan toon bàay mooŋ khráp_) – See you at 1:00 PM.

---

#### 3. ทุ่ม (_thûm_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Night o'clock.
2. **Meaning**: Time word used for evening hours from 7:00 PM (1 ทุ่ม) through 11:00 PM (5 ทุ่ม).
3. **Word Breakdown**: **ทุ่ม** (_thûm_) – Falling tone.
4. **Cultural & Historical Context**: Originates from the deep, heavy sound (_thum_) of large traditional drums beaten during night watches in old Siam to announce evening hours.
5. **Modern Usage**: Used daily when arranging evening dinners, movies, or night outings.
6. **Formality**: Neutral to Casual.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **เราจะไปดูหนังตอนสองทุ่มครับ** (_raw ca pay duu năŋ toon sɔ̌ɔŋ thûm khráp_) – We will go watch a movie at 8:00 PM.
8. **Synonyms**: **นาฬิกา** (_naa-li-kaa_ – formal).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **ผมจะกลับบ้านตอนสองทุ่มครับ** (_phŏm ca klàp báan toon sɔ̌ɔŋ thûm khráp_) – I will go home at 8:00 PM.

---

#### 4. ตี (_thii_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Strike / Hit (overnight hour marker).
2. **Meaning**: Prefix used for overnight and early morning hours from 1:00 AM (ตีหนึ่ง) to 5:00 AM (ตีห้า).
3. **Word Breakdown**: **ตี** (_thii_) – Mid tone.
4. **Cultural & Historical Context**: Comes from night watchmen striking metal gongs or hollow bamboo chimes (_ตี_) on hourly patrols through towns during the late night.
5. **Modern Usage**: Used when talking about late-night studying, early flights, or wake-up times.
6. **Formality**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **ผมตื่นนอนตอนตีห้าครับ** (_phŏm tUun nɔɔn toon thii hâa khráp_) – I wake up at 5:00 AM.
8. **Synonyms**: N/A.
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **เขานอนตอนตีสองครับ** (_khaw nɔɔn toon thii sɔ̌ɔŋ khráp_) – He goes to sleep at 2:00 AM.

---

#### 5. ครึ่ง (_khrÛŋ_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Half / Half-past.
2. **Meaning**: Placed after any hour mark to signify 30 minutes past the hour.
3. **Word Breakdown**: **ครึ่ง** (_khrÛŋ_) – Falling tone.
4. **Cultural & Historical Context**: The standard Thai term for "half" across all measurements (time, weights, fractions).
5. **Modern Usage**: Appended to time phrases to indicate half-past.
6. **Formality**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **เราจะพบกันตอนเที่ยงครึ่งครับ** (_raw ca phóp kan toon thîaŋ khrÛŋ khráp_) – We will meet at 12:30 PM.
8. **Synonyms**: **สามสิบนาที** (_săam sìp naa-thii_ – 30 minutes).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **ผมมีเรียนตอนเก้าโมงครึ่งครับ** (_phŏm mii rian toon kâaw mooŋ khrÛŋ khráp_) – I have class at 9:30 AM.
