### Exercise 1: Practice Reading Words with ไ- and ใ-

#### 1. ไปไหน (_pay náy_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Go + where
2. **Meaning**: "Where are you going?" or "Where to go?". In Thai culture, asking "ไปไหน" functions as a standard friendly greeting when encountering someone.
3. **Word Breakdown**:
    - **ไป** (_pay_)
        - _Phonetic_: _pay_ (Mid tone, spelled with **ไ-**)
        - _4. English Meaning & Context_: Go (motion away from speaker).
        - _5. Daily Usage_: Used constantly when moving or heading anywhere.
        - _6. Formality_: Neutral.
        - _7. Daily Example_: **ผมจะไปทำงาน** (_phŏm ca pay tham-ŋaan_) – I am going to work.
        - _8. Synonyms_: **ไปยัง** (_pay yaŋ_ – formal/literary).
    - **ไหน** (_náy_)
        - _Phonetic_: _náy_ (Rising tone, spelled with **ไ-**)
        - _4. English Meaning & Context_: Where / Which.
        - _5. Daily Usage_: Standard question word for asking location or choices.
        - _6. Formality_: Neutral.
        - _7. Daily Example_: **บ้านคุณอยู่ไหน** (_báan khun yùu náy_) – Where is your house?
        - _8. Synonyms_: **ที่ไหน** (_thîi náy_ – at where).
4. **Thai Conversational Version**: **คุณจะไปไหนครับ** (_khun ca pay náy khráp_) – Where are you going?

---

#### 2. ไปไกล (_pay klay_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Go + far
2. **Meaning**: To go far away or to a distant place.
3. **Word Breakdown**:
    - **ไป** (_pay_): Go. Formality: Neutral.
    - **ไกล** (_klay_)
        - _Phonetic_: _klay_ (Mid tone, spelled with **ไ-**)
        - _4. English Meaning & Context_: Far / Distant.
        - _5. Daily Usage_: Used when describing physical distances.
        - _6. Formality_: Neutral.
        - _7. Daily Example_: **บ้านผมอยู่ไม่ไกล** (_báan phŏm yùu mây klay_) – My house is not far.
        - _8. Synonyms_: **ห่างไกล** (_hàaŋ klay_ – distant/remote).
4. **Thai Conversational Version**: **อย่าไปไกลนะครับ** (_yàa pay klay ná khráp_) – Please don't go far.

---

#### 3. อะไร (_?a ray_)

1. **Literal Translation**: What
2. **Meaning**: What / Anything. The foundational Thai question word for inquiring about objects, actions, or choices.
3. **Word Breakdown**:
    - **อะ** (_?a_): Unstressed prefix.
    - **ไร** (_ray_)
        - _Phonetic_: _ray_ (Mid tone, spelled with **ไ-**)
        - _4. English Meaning & Context_: Element meaning "what".
        - _5. Daily Usage_: Universal in questions, ordering food, or asking for clarification.
        - _6. Formality_: Neutral.
        - _7. Daily Example_: **คุณต้องการอะไรครับ** (_khun tɔ̂ɔŋ-kaan ?a ray khráp_) – What do you need?
        - _8. Synonyms_: **สิ่งใด** (_sìŋ day_ – formal/literary).
4. **Thai Conversational Version**: **คุณชอบทานอะไรครับ** (_khun chɔ̂ɔp thaan ?a ray khráp_) – What do you like to eat?

---

#### 4. ในใจ (_nay cay_)

1. **Literal Translation**: In + heart/mind
2. **Meaning**: In one's heart or mind (inner thoughts or personal feelings).
3. **Word Breakdown**:
    - **ใน** (_nay_)
        - _Phonetic_: _nay_ (Mid tone, spelled with **ใ-**)
        - _4. English Meaning & Context_: In / Inside.
        - _5. Daily Usage_: Used daily for spatial locations or internal states.
        - _6. Formality_: Neutral.
        - _7. Daily Example_: **อยู่ในบ้าน** (_yùu nay báan_) – Inside the house.
        - _8. Synonyms_: **ข้างใน** (_khâaŋ nay_).
    - **ใจ** (_cay_)
        - _Phonetic_: _cay_ (Mid tone, spelled with **ใ-**)
        - _4. English Meaning & Context_: Heart / Mind. In Thai culture, **ใจ** is considered the center of emotions and character.
        - _5. Daily Usage_: Root word for dozens of emotional compound words.
        - _6. Formality_: Neutral.
        - _7. Daily Example_: **เขาเป็นคนใจดี** (_khaw pen khon cay dii_) – He is a kind-hearted person.
        - _8. Synonyms_: **หัวใจ** (_hŭa cay_ – physical heart/soul).
4. **Thai Conversational Version**: **เขาคิดอะไรอยู่ในใจครับ** (_khaw khít ?a ray yùu nay cay khráp_) – What is he thinking in his mind?

---

#### 5. พบใคร (_phóp khray_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Meet + who
2. **Meaning**: To meet someone, or asking who someone is meeting.
3. **Word Breakdown**:
    - **พบ** (_phóp_)
        - _Phonetic_: _phóp_ (High tone)

        - _5. Daily Usage_: Used when arranging appointments or meeting people.
        - _6. Formality_: Neutral to polite.
        - _7. Daily Example_: **ผมอยากพบอาจารย์** (_phŏm yàak phóp ?aa-caan_) – I want to meet the professor.
        - _8. Synonyms_: **เจอ** (_cəə_ – casual everyday "meet").
    - **ใคร** (_khray_)
        - _Phonetic_: _khray_ (Mid tone, spelled with **ใ-**)
        - _4. English Meaning & Context_: Who / Whom.
        - _5. Daily Usage_: Standard question word for people.
        - _6. Formality_: Neutral.
        - _7. Daily Example_: **คุณมากับใครครับ** (_khun maa kàp khray khráp_) – Who did you come with?
        - _8. Synonyms_: **ผู้ใด** (_phûu day_ – formal).
4. **Thai Conversational Version**: **คุณมาพบใครครับ** (_khun maa phóp khray khráp_) – Who have you come to meet?

#### 8. ดีไหม (_dii máy_)

4. **Thai Conversational Version**: **ไปทานข้าวเที่ยงกัน ดีไหมครับ** (_pay thaan khâaw thîaŋ kan, dii máy khráp_) – Shall we go eat lunch together, is that good?

### Exercise 2: Writing Thai Script (Items 1–4 on Page 33)

#### 1. tham dii -> **ทำดี** (_tham dii_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Do + good
2. **Meaning**: To do good deeds or behave well.
3. **Word Breakdown**:
    - **ทำ** (_tham_)
        - _Phonetic_: _tham_ (Mid tone, uses **สระอำ** **-ำ**)
        - _4. English Meaning & Context_: Do / Make.
        - _5. Daily Usage_: Core action verb used every day.
        - _6. Formality_: Neutral.
        - _7. Daily Example_: **คุณกำลังทำอะไร** (_khun kam-laŋ tham ?a ray_) – What are you doing?
        - _8. Synonyms_: **ปฏิบัติ** (_pa-ti-bàt_ – formal perform/execute).
    - **ดี** (_dii_): Good / Well. Formality: Neutral.
4. **Thai Conversational Version**: **เราควรทำดีต่อกันครับ** (_raw khuan tham dii tɔ̀ɔ kan khráp_) – We should do good to each other.

---

#### 2. raw ram -> **เรารำ** (_raw ram_)

1. **Literal Translation**: We + dance (traditional Thai dance)
2. **Meaning**: We dance traditional Thai classical/folk dance.
3. **Word Breakdown**:
    - **เรา** (_raw_): We / Us (uses **สระเอา** **เ-า**). Formality: Neutral.
    - **รำ** (_ram_)
        - _Phonetic_: _ram_ (Mid tone, uses **สระอำ** **-ำ**)
        - _4. English Meaning & Context_: Traditional Thai dance.
        - _5. Daily Usage_: Refers specifically to graceful Thai classical or folk dance styles.
        - _6. Formality_: Neutral.
        - _7. Daily Example_: **เธอชอบรำไทย** (_thəə chɔ̂ɔp ram thay_) – She likes Thai dancing.
        - _8. Synonyms_: **เต้นรำ** (_tên ram_ – general ballroom/social dancing).
4. **Thai Conversational Version**: **พวกเรารำไทยด้วยกันครับ** (_phûak raw ram thay dûay kan khráp_) – We perform Thai dance together.

---

#### 3. ka phraw -> **กะเพรา** (_ka phraw_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Holy basil
2. **Meaning**: Holy basil (uses **สระเอา** **เ-า** in **เพรา**). Key herb in Thai stir-fries like **ผัดกะเพรา**.
3. **Thai Conversational Version**: **ขอผัดกะเพราไก่ไข่ดาวครับ** (_khɔ̌ɔ phàt ka phraw kày khày daaw khráp_) – I would like chicken holy basil stir-fry with a fried egg.

---

#### 4. kra păw -> **กระเป๋า** (_kra păw_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Bag / Purse / Wallet
2. **Meaning**: A bag or wallet (uses **สระเอา** **เ-า** in **เป๋า**).
3. **Thai Conversational Version**: **กระเป๋าใบนี้สวยมากครับ** (_kra păw bay níi sŭay mâak khráp_) – This bag is very beautiful.

### Part 2: Exercise 3 (Reading Aloud, Items 1–8 on Page 34)

### Detailed Key Vocabulary Breakdown

#### 1. โมง (_mooŋ_)

1. **Literal Translation**: O'clock / Daytime hour.
2. **Meaning**: Standard classifier used for telling daytime hours between 6:00 AM and 5:59 PM.
3. **Word Breakdown**: **โมง** (_mooŋ_) – Mid tone.
4. **Cultural & Historical Context**: Historically derived from the resonant sound of large gongs (_mooŋ...mooŋ_) struck in village towers in ancient Siam to signal daytime hours.
5. **Modern Usage**: Used constantly in daily life when asking or telling daytime time.
6. **Formality**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **ตอนนี้กี่โมงแล้วครับ** (_toon níi kìi mooŋ lɛ́ɛw khráp_) – What time is it now?
8. **Synonyms**: **นาฬิกา** (_naa-li-kaa_ – formal/official o'clock).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **ตอนนี้แปดโมงเช้าครับ** (_toon níi pɛ̀ɛt mooŋ cháaw khráp_) – It is 8:00 AM now.

---

#### 2. บ่าย (_bàay_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Afternoon / Post-noon.
2. **Meaning**: Time prefix used exclusively for afternoon hours from 1:00 PM through 3:59 PM.
3. **Word Breakdown**: **บ่าย** (_bàay_) – Low tone.
4. **Cultural & Historical Context**: Refers to the sun shifting past its peak position into the afternoon sky.
5. **Modern Usage**: Essential for setting afternoon appointments, classes, or lunch plans.
6. **Formality**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **ผมมีเรียนตอนบ่ายสองโมงครับ** (_phŏm mii rian toon bàay sɔ̌ɔŋ mooŋ khráp_) – I have class at 2:00 PM.
8. **Synonyms**: **ช่วงบ่าย** (_chûaŋ bàay_ – afternoon period).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **เจอกันตอนบ่ายโมงครับ** (_cəə kan toon bàay mooŋ khráp_) – See you at 1:00 PM.

---

#### 3. ทุ่ม (_thûm_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Night o'clock.
2. **Meaning**: Time word used for evening hours from 7:00 PM (1 ทุ่ม) through 11:00 PM (5 ทุ่ม).
3. **Word Breakdown**: **ทุ่ม** (_thûm_) – Falling tone.
4. **Cultural & Historical Context**: Originates from the deep, heavy sound (_thum_) of large traditional drums beaten during night watches in old Siam to announce evening hours.
5. **Modern Usage**: Used daily when arranging evening dinners, movies, or night outings.
6. **Formality**: Neutral to Casual.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **เราจะไปดูหนังตอนสองทุ่มครับ** (_raw ca pay duu năŋ toon sɔ̌ɔŋ thûm khráp_) – We will go watch a movie at 8:00 PM.
8. **Synonyms**: **นาฬิกา** (_naa-li-kaa_ – formal).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **ผมจะกลับบ้านตอนสองทุ่มครับ** (_phŏm ca klàp báan toon sɔ̌ɔŋ thûm khráp_) – I will go home at 8:00 PM.

---

#### 4. ตี (_thii_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Strike / Hit (overnight hour marker).
2. **Meaning**: Prefix used for overnight and early morning hours from 1:00 AM (ตีหนึ่ง) to 5:00 AM (ตีห้า).
3. **Word Breakdown**: **ตี** (_thii_) – Mid tone.
4. **Cultural & Historical Context**: Comes from night watchmen striking metal gongs or hollow bamboo chimes (_ตี_) on hourly patrols through towns during the late night.
5. **Modern Usage**: Used when talking about late-night studying, early flights, or wake-up times.
6. **Formality**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **ผมตื่นนอนตอนตีห้าครับ** (_phŏm tUun nɔɔn toon thii hâa khráp_) – I wake up at 5:00 AM.
8. **Synonyms**: N/A.
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **เขานอนตอนตีสองครับ** (_khaw nɔɔn toon thii sɔ̌ɔŋ khráp_) – He goes to sleep at 2:00 AM.

---

#### 5. ครึ่ง (_khrÛŋ_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Half / Half-past.
2. **Meaning**: Placed after any hour mark to signify 30 minutes past the hour.
3. **Word Breakdown**: **ครึ่ง** (_khrÛŋ_) – Falling tone.
4. **Cultural & Historical Context**: The standard Thai term for "half" across all measurements (time, weights, fractions).
5. **Modern Usage**: Appended to time phrases to indicate half-past.
6. **Formality**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **เราจะพบกันตอนเที่ยงครึ่งครับ** (_raw ca phóp kan toon thîaŋ khrÛŋ khráp_) – We will meet at 12:30 PM.
8. **Synonyms**: **สามสิบนาที** (_săam sìp naa-thii_ – 30 minutes).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **ผมมีเรียนตอนเก้าโมงครึ่งครับ** (_phŏm mii rian toon kâaw mooŋ khrÛŋ khráp_) – I have class at 9:30 AM.
