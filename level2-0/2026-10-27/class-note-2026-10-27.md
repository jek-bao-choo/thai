### Part 1: Exercise 4 Continuation (Items 8–10)

#### Item 8: ตัดแปะ (_tàt-pɛ̀_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Cut + paste / stick.
2. **Meaning**: "Cut and paste" or "crafting with paper cuts."
3. **Word Breakdown**:
    - **ตัด** (_tàt_) – Low tone
        - _4. English Meaning & Context_: Cut (with scissors or knife).
        - _5. Daily Usage_: Used daily when cutting hair, paper, or food.
        - _6. Formality_: Neutral.
        - _7. Daily Example_: **ผมอยากตัดผมครับ** (_phŏm yàak tàt phŏm khráp_) – I want to get a haircut.
        - _8. Synonyms_: **หั่น** (_hàn_ – chop/slice).
    - **แปะ** (_pɛ̀_) – Low tone
        - _4. English Meaning & Context_: Paste / Stick / Attach.
        - _5. Daily Usage_: Used when sticking tape, posters, or notes.
        - _6. Formality_: Casual to Neutral.
        - _7. Daily Example_: **ช่วยแปะกระดาษบนผนังหน่อยครับ** (_chûay pɛ̀ kra-dàat bon pha-năŋ nɔ̀oy khráp_) – Please stick the paper on the wall.
        - _8. Synonyms_: **ติด** (_tìt_ – stick/attach).
4. **Thai Conversational Version**: **เด็กๆ ชอบตัดแปะกระดาษครับ** (_dèk-dèk chɔ̂ɔp tàt-pɛ̀ kra-dàat khráp_) – Children like doing paper cut and paste crafts.

#### Item 9: จัดแจก (_càt-cɛɛk_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Arrange + distribute.
2. **Meaning**: "To distribute", "to hand out", or "to organize and pass out."
3. **Word Breakdown**:
    - **จัด** (_càt_) – Low tone: Organize / Arrange. Formality: Neutral.
    - **แจก** (_cɛɛk_) – Low tone: Distribute / Hand out. Formality: Neutral.
        - _4. Context_: Commonly used when distributing handouts, gifts, or meals.
        - _5. Daily Usage_: Used in classroom or office settings.
        - _7. Daily Example_: **อาจารย์แจกข้อสอบครับ** (_?aa-caan cɛɛk khɔ̂ɔ-sɔ̂ɔp khráp_) – The professor handed out the exam paper.

4. **Thai Conversational Version**: **ช่วยจัดแจกเอกสารให้ทุกคนด้วยครับ** (_chûay càt-cɛɛk ?èek-ka-săan hây thúk khon dûay khráp_) – Please distribute the documents to everyone.

2. **Meaning**: "Toi is a second-year student (sophomore)."

5. **Daily Usage**: Used when stating your academic year.
6. **Formality**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example**: **ผมเป็นนักเรียนปีสองครับ** (_phŏm pen nák-rian pii sɔ̌ɔŋ khráp_) – I am a second-year student.

1. **Literal Translation**: He/She + study + Korea + with + Chinese Mandarin.
2. **Meaning**: "He/She studies Korean and Mandarin Chinese."
3. **Word Breakdown**:
    - **เขา** (_khaw_): He / She / They (Rising tone). Neutral.
    - **เรียน** (_rian_): Study (Mid tone). Neutral.

    - **กับ** (_kàp_): With / And (Low tone). Neutral.

4. **Context & English Meaning**: **จีนกลาง** combines **จีน** (_Chinese_) + **กลาง** (_central_), denoting official Mandarin rather than regional dialects like Teochew or Cantonese.
5. **Daily Usage**: Standard term when referring to Mandarin language courses.
6. **Formality**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example**: **ผมอยากเรียนภาษาจีนกลางครับ** (_phŏm yàak rian phaa-săa ciin-klaaŋ khráp_) – I want to learn Mandarin Chinese.
8. **Synonyms**: **ภาษาจีนมาตรฐาน** (_phaa-săa ciin maa-tra-thăan_ – formal standard Chinese).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **เขาเรียนภาษาเกาหลีกับจีนกลางครับ** (_khaw rian phaa-săa kaw-lĭi kàp ciin-klaaŋ khráp_)

### Question 1: **ต้อย และ อ้อย เรียน อะไร**

#### 1. Literal Translation

Toi + and + Oi + study + what.

#### 2. Meaning

"What do Toi and Oi study?"

#### 3. Word Breakdown with Phonetic Pronunciation

- **ต้อย** (_tɔ̂ɔy_): Proper name Toi (Falling tone).
- **และ** (_lɛ́_): And (High tone).
- **อ้อย** (_?ɔ̂ɔy_): Proper name Oi (Falling tone).
- **เรียน** (_rian_): Study / Learn (Mid tone).
- **อะไร** (_?a-ray_): What (Mid tone).

#### 4. English Meaning, Cultural & Historical Context

- **เรียน** (_rian_): Foundational Thai verb for studying or attending classes.
- **อะไร** (_?a-ray_): Universal question word for "what".

#### 5. Modern Usage in Daily Conversation

- **เรียน**: Used daily when asking or telling what subject, course, or language someone is taking.
- **อะไร**: Used constantly across all casual and polite settings.

#### 6. Formality Level

- Neutral.

#### 7. Daily Example Sentence

- **คุณเรียนภาษาอะไรครับ** (_khun rian phaa-săa ?a-ray khráp_) – What language are you studying?

#### 8. Synonyms

- **เรียน**: **ศึกษา** (_sUk-săa_ – formal/academic study).
- **และ**: **กับ** (_kàp_ – casual "with/and").

#### 9. Thai Conversational Version & Answer

- **Question**: **ต้อยและอ้อยเรียนอะไรครับ** (_tɔ̂ɔy lɛ́ ?ɔ̂ɔy rian ?a-ray khráp_)
- **Textbook Answer**: **พวกเขารู้จักเรียนภาษาเกาหลีกับจีนกลางครับ** (_phûak-khaw rian phaa-săa kaw-lĭi kàp ciin-klaaŋ khráp_) – They study Korean and Mandarin Chinese.

### Question 2: **ใคร พูด เกาหลี และ จีนกลาง ได้ เก่ง กว่ากัน**

#### 1. Literal Translation

Who + speak + Korea + and + Mandarin Chinese + can + skilled + more than each other.

#### 2. Meaning

"Who speaks Korean and Mandarin better?"

#### 3. Word Breakdown with Phonetic Pronunciation

- **ใคร** (_khray_): Who (Mid tone).
- **พูด** (_phûat_): Speak (Falling tone).
- **เกาหลี** (_kaw-lĭi_): Korea / Korean (Mid + Rising tone).
- **จีนกลาง** (_ciin-klaaŋ_): Mandarin Chinese (Mid + Mid tone).
- **เก่งกว่ากัน** (_kèŋ kwàa kan_): Skilled + more than + each other = Better than one another (Low + Low + Mid tone).

#### 4. English Meaning, Cultural & Historical Context

- **จีนกลาง** (_ciin-klaaŋ_): Combines **จีน** (_Chinese_) + **กลาง** (_central_), referring specifically to standard Mandarin rather than regional dialects.
- **เก่งกว่ากัน** (_kèŋ kwàa kan_): Comparative phrase used to ask who is better at a skill between two people.

#### 5. Modern Usage in Daily Conversation

- Used when comparing skills, language fluency, or sports ability between peers.

#### 6. Formality Level

- Neutral.

#### 7. Daily Example Sentence

- **ใครพูดภาษาไทยเก่งกว่ากันครับ** (_khray phûat phaa-săa thay kèŋ kwàa kan khráp_) – Who speaks Thai better?

#### 8. Synonyms

- **พูด**: **กล่าว** (_klàaw_ – formal state/say).
- **เก่ง**: **ชำนาญ** (_cham-naan_ – proficient/expert).

#### 9. Thai Conversational Version & Answer

- **Question**: **ใครพูดภาษาเกาหลีและจีนกลางได้เก่งกว่ากันครับ** (_khray phûat phaa-săa kaw-lĭi lɛ́ ciin-klaaŋ dâay kèŋ kwàa kan khráp_)
- **Textbook Answer**: **ต้อยพูดได้เก่งกว่าครับ** (_tɔ̂ɔy phûat dâay kèŋ kwàa khráp_) – Toi speaks better.

### Core Structure: **ถ้ายั้งงั้น** (_thâa yaŋŋán_)

1. **Literal Translation**: If + like that / in that manner.
2. **Meaning**: "In that case...", "If so...", or "Then...".
3. **Word Breakdown**:
    - **ถ้า** (_thâa_)
        - _4. Context_: Conditional conjunction ("if").
        - _5. Usage_: Standard daily word for introducing conditional situations.
        - _6. Formality_: Neutral.
    - **ยั้งงั้น** (_yaŋŋán_)
        - _4. Context_: Conversational contraction of formal **อย่างนั้น** (_yàaŋ-nán_ = in that manner). In Thai social culture, using this phrase allows a speaker to respond empathetically, offer a favor, or adapt plans smoothly after receiving new information.
        - _5. Usage_: Placed at the start of a sentence when suggesting an alternative, offering a favor, or giving advice based on what was just said.
        - _6. Formality_: Casual to Neutral (spoken Thai).
4. **Daily Example Sentence**: **ถ้ายั้งงั้น เราไปทานข้าวกันเลยครับ** (_thâa yaŋŋán, raw pay thaan khâaw kan ləəy khráp_) – In that case, let's go eat right now!
5. **Synonyms**: **ถ้าอย่างนั้น** (_thâa yàaŋ-nán_ – formal/polite), **งั้น** (_ŋán_ – casual short form).
6. **Thai Conversational Version**: **ถ้ายั้งงั้น คุณพักผ่อนเยอะๆ นะครับ** (_thâa yaŋŋán khun phákphɔ̀ɔn yə́ yə́ ná khráp_) – In that case, rest up a lot!

### Core Structural Distinction

- **ขอ** (_khɔ̂ɔ_): Used when asking for permission or requesting an item or service directly for yourself (e.g., ordering food, asking a question).
- **ช่วย** (_chûay_): Used when asking someone else to perform an action or do a favor for you or another person (e.g., telling someone a message, buying something for you).

---

### Step-by-Step Breakdown of Key Target Grammar Words

#### 1. **ขอ** (_khɔ̂ɔ_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Request / Ask for / May I have.
2. **Meaning**: "May I have...", "Can I ask...", or "I would like to request...". Used to ask for items, services, or permission for yourself.
3. **Word Breakdown**:
    - **ขอ** (_khɔ̂ɔ_ – Rising tone)
4. **Context & English Meaning**: Essential polite request prefix in Thai social etiquette. Adding **ขอ** softens requests and demonstrates politeness when ordering or asking permission.
5. **Modern Daily Usage**: Used constantly every day when ordering food at restaurants, asking for the check, or seeking permission to enter/leave a room.
6. **Formality**: Neutral to Polite.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **ขอกาแฟเย็นแก้วหนึ่งครับ** (_khɔ̌ɔ kaa-fɛɛ yen kɛ̂ɛw nUuŋ khráp_) – May I have one glass of iced coffee, please.
8. **Synonyms**: **ขอร้อง** (_khɔ̂ɔ-rɔ́ɔŋ_ – beg/plead), **วาน** (_waan_ – ask a favor/casual).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **ขอถามอะไรหน่อยครับ** (_khɔ̌ɔ thăam ?a-ray nɔ̀oy khráp_) – May I ask you something, please?

---

#### 2. **ช่วย** (_chûay_)

4. **English Meaning, Cultural & Historical Context**:
    - **ถ้า**: Conditional conjunction ("if").
    - **ยั้งงั้น**: Conversational spoken contraction of **อย่างนั้น**. In Thai social etiquette, **ถ้ายั้งงั้น** is an empathetic transition used when adapting plans or offering help based on new information.
5. **Modern Daily Usage**: Used constantly in conversation when suggesting an alternative, offering a favor, or giving advice based on what someone just said.
6. **Formality**: Casual to Neutral (spoken Thai).
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **ถ้ายั้งงั้น เราไปทานข้าวกันเลยครับ** (_thâa yaŋŋán, raw pay thaan khâaw kan ləəy khráp_) – In that case, let's go eat right now!
8. **Synonyms**: **ถ้าอย่างนั้น** (_thâa yàaŋ-nán_ – polite/formal), **งั้น** (_ŋán_ – casual short form).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **ถ้ายั้งงั้น คุณพักผ่อนเยอะๆ นะครับ** (_thâa yaŋŋán khun phákphɔ̀ɔn yə́ yə́ ná khráp_) – In that case, rest up a lot!

### Step-by-Step Word Breakdown for Target Vocabulary

#### 1. **ตลาด** (_talàat_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Market.
2. **Meaning**: Wet market / Fresh produce market.
3. **Word Breakdown**: **ต** (_ta_ – Low short tone) + **ลาด** (_làat_ – Low tone). Phonetic: _ta-làat_.
4. **Context & English Meaning**: Refers to traditional Thai open-air fresh markets (**ตลาดสด** _talàat sòt_). Historically, daily Thai life revolved around fresh river and land markets for seasonal herbs, fruits, and fresh meats.
5. **Modern Daily Usage**: Used daily when talking about buying fresh ingredients, street food, or local produce.
6. **Formality**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **คุณแม่ชอบไปซื้อผักที่ตลาดสดครับ** (_khun mɛ̂ɛ chɔ̂ɔp pay sÚu phàk thîi ta-làat sòt khráp_) – My mother likes to buy vegetables at the fresh market.

#### 2. **ซุปเปอร์มาร์เก็ต** (_súppəəmaakèt_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Supermarket.
2. **Meaning**: Supermarket / Grocery store.
3. **Word Breakdown**: **ซุป** (_súp_ – High) + **เปอร์** (_pəə_ – Mid) + **มาร์** (_maa_ – Mid) + **เก็ต** (_kèt_ – Low). Phonetic: _súp-pəə-maa-kèt_.

5. **Modern Daily Usage**: Used constantly in city life when purchasing imported goods, packaged foods, and toiletries.
6. **Formality**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **ผมจะไปซื้อนมที่ซุปเปอร์มาร์เก็ตครับ** (_phŏm ca pay sÚu nom thîi súp-pəə-maa-kèt khráp_) – I am going to buy milk at the supermarket.

9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **ปกติคุณไปซุปเปอร์มาร์เก็ตบ่อยไหมครับ** (_pok-ka-ti khun pay súp-pəə-maa-kèt bɔ̀ɔy máy khráp_) – Normally, do you go to the supermarket often?

#### 3. **ปกติ** (_pokkati_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Normal / Usual.
2. **Meaning**: Normally / Usually / Generally.
3. **Word Breakdown**: **ปก** (_pok_ – Low) + **ติ** (_ka-ti_ – Low + Low). Phonetic: _pok-ka-ti_.
4. **Context & English Meaning**: Originates from Sanskrit/Pali (_prakṛti_). Used as an adverb to describe general habits, default routines, or typical behaviors.
5. **Modern Daily Usage**: Placed at the beginning of sentences to describe what you usually do.
6. **Formality**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **ปกติผมกินข้าวเช้าที่บ้านครับ** (_pok-ka-ti phŏm kin khâaw cháaw thîi báan khráp_) – Normally I eat breakfast at home.
8. **Synonyms**: **โดยทั่วไป** (_dooy thûa-pay_ – generally), **ตามปกติ** (_taam pok-ka-ti_ – as usual).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **ปกติคุณซื้อของที่ไหนครับ** (_pok-ka-ti khun sÚu khɔ̌ɔŋ thîi năy khráp_) – Normally, where do you buy your things?

#### 4. **ของใช้ในห้องน้ำ** (_khɔ́ɔŋcháy nay hɔ̂ɔŋnáam_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Things/Goods + use + in + room + water = Bathroom toiletries.
2. **Meaning**: Bathroom toiletries / Personal care items.
3. **Word Breakdown**:
    - **ของใช้** (_khɔ́ɔŋ-cháy_ – Rising + High tone): Household supplies/items.
    - **ใน** (_nay_ – Mid tone): In / Inside.
    - **ห้องน้ำ** (_hɔ̂ɔŋ-náam_ – Falling + High tone): Restroom / Bathroom.
4. **Context & English Meaning**: Compound noun encompassing soap, shampoo, toothbrushes, toothpaste, and bath supplies.
5. **Modern Daily Usage**: Used when grouping personal care items during grocery shopping trips.
6. **Formality**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **ผมต้องไปซื้อของใช้ในห้องน้ำครับ** (_phŏm tɔ̂ɔŋ pay sÚu khɔ́ɔŋ-cháy nay hɔ̂ɔŋ-náam khráp_) – I need to go buy bathroom supplies.

9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **ขอซื้อของใช้ในห้องน้ำหน่อยครับ** (_khɔ̌ɔ sÚu khɔ́ɔŋ-cháy nay hɔ̂ɔŋ-náam nɔ̀oy khráp_) – May I buy some bathroom supplies, please.

#### 5. **อาหารทะเล** (_?aahăan thalee_)

2. **Meaning**: Seafood.
3. **Word Breakdown**:

7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **ผมชอบทานอาหารทะเลมากครับ** (_phŏm chɔ̂ɔp thaan ?aa-hăan tha-lee mâak khráp_) – I like eating seafood very much.

9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **ร้านนี้มีอาหารทะเลสดมากครับ** (_ráan níi mii ?aa-hăan tha-lee sòt mâak khráp_) – This restaurant has very fresh seafood.
