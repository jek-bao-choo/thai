### Detailed Step-by-Step Vocabulary Breakdown

#### 1. **ข้าว** (_khâaw_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Rice / Food / Grain.
2. **Meaning**: Cooked rice, grain, or food/meal in general.
3. **Word Breakdown**:
    - **ข้าว** (_khâaw_)
    - _Phonetic_: _khâaw_ (Falling tone, Initial High Consonant **ข** + long vowel **-าว** + Mai Tho **-้**)
4. **English Meaning, Cultural & Historical Context**: Rice is the defining cultural staple of Thailand. In Thai culture, asking if someone has eaten rice (**ทานข้าวหรือยัง**) is the standard daily greeting equivalent to "How are you?".
5. **Modern Daily Usage**: Used multiple times every day during meal times (**ทานข้าว** _eat a meal_, **ข้าวเช้า** _breakfast_, **ข้าวเย็น** _dinner_).
6. **Formality**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **คุณทานข้าวหรือยังครับ** (_khun thaan khâaw hUu yaŋ khráp_) – Have you eaten yet?
8. **Synonyms**: **อาหาร** (_?aa-hăan_ – formal food/meal).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **ไปทานข้าวด้วยกันไหมครับ** (_pay thaan khâaw dûay-kan máy khráp_) – Shall we go eat together?

---

#### 2. **ถ้า** (_thâa_)

1. **Literal Translation**: If.
2. **Meaning**: Conditional conjunction "if".
3. **Word Breakdown**:
    - **ถ้า** (_thâa_)
    - _Phonetic_: _thâa_ (Falling tone, Initial High Consonant **ถ** + long vowel **-า** + Mai Tho **-้**)
4. **English Meaning, Cultural & Historical Context**: Core conditional conjunction used to set up conditions, plans, or polite invitations.
5. **Modern Daily Usage**: Used constantly in daily conversation when making plans or setting conditions (**ถ้าว่าง** _if free_).
6. **Formality**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **ถ้าพรุ่งนี้ว่าง เราไปทานข้าวกันนะครับ** (_thâa phrùŋ-níi wâaŋ raw pay thaan khâaw kan ná khráp_) – If you are free tomorrow, let me know and we can eat together!
8. **Synonyms**: **หาก** (_hàak_ – formal "if").
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **ถ้าว่าง เจอกันนะครับ** (_thâa wâaŋ cəə kan ná khráp_) – If you're free, let's meet up!

---

#### 3. **ให้** (_hây_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Give / For / Allow.
2. **Meaning**: Preposition "for" (someone) or verb "to give".
3. **Word Breakdown**:
    - **ให้** (_hây_)
    - _Phonetic_: _hây_ (Falling tone, Initial High Consonant **ห** + special vowel **ใ-** + Mai Tho **-้**)
4. **English Meaning, Cultural & Historical Context**: Reflects the Thai value of _Nam Jai_ (generosity/helpfulness). Placed after action verbs to indicate doing something for someone else's benefit.
5. **Modern Daily Usage**: Used daily when buying things for friends (**ซื้อให้**), sending items (**ส่งให้**), or ordering food for others (**สั่งให้**).
6. **Formality**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **ผมซื้อกาแฟมาให้ครับ** (_phŏm sUu kaa-fɛɛ maa hây khráp_) – I bought coffee for you.
8. **Synonyms**: **มอบให้** (_mɔ̂ɔp hây_ – formal present/bestow).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **เดี๋ยวผมสั่งอาหารให้ครูนะครับ** (_dĭaw phŏm sàŋ ?aa-hăan hây khruu ná khráp_) – I will order food for you in a moment!

---

#### 4. **เสื้อ** (_sÛa_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Shirt / Upper garment.
2. **Meaning**: Shirt, blouse, top, or clothing (**เสื้อผ้า**).
3. **Word Breakdown**:
    - **เสื้อ** (_sÛa_)
    - _Phonetic_: _sÛa_ (Falling tone, Initial High Consonant **ส** + diphthong **เ-ือ** + Mai Tho **-้**)
4. **English Meaning, Cultural & Historical Context**: Essential attire noun used across all clothing and shopping contexts.
5. **Modern Daily Usage**: Used daily when dressing or shopping (**ซื้อเสื้อใหม่** _buy a new shirt_).
6. **Formality**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **วันนี้คุณใส่เสื้อสวยมากครับ** (_wan-níi khun sày sÛa sŭay mâak khráp_) – Today you are wearing a very nice shirt!
8. **Synonyms**: **เสื้อผ้า** (_sÛa-phâa_ – clothes/apparel).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **เสื้อตัวนี้สวยมากครับ** (_sÛa tua níi sŭay mâak khráp_) – This shirt is very beautiful.

---

#### 5. **สั้น** (_sân_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Short (length).
2. **Meaning**: Short in physical length or duration.
3. **Word Breakdown**:
    - **สั้น** (_sân_)
    - _Phonetic_: _sân_ (Falling tone, Initial High Consonant **ส** + short vowel **-ะ** + final **น** + Mai Tho **-้**)
4. **English Meaning, Cultural & Historical Context**: Describes length specifically (unlike **เตี้ย** _tîa_, which means short in height).
5. **Modern Daily Usage**: Used when discussing clothing (**กางเกงขาสั้น** _shorts_), hair length, or brief time periods.
6. **Formality**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **เอากางเกงขาสั้นไปด้วยนะครับ** (_?aw kaaŋ-keeŋ khăa sân pay dûay ná khráp_) – Remember to bring shorts along too!
8. **Synonyms**: N/A (specific length descriptor).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **วันนี้อากาศร้อน ใส่กางเกงขาสั้นดีกว่าครับ** (_wan-níi ?aa-kàat rɔ́ɔn, sày kaaŋ-keeŋ khăa sân dii kwàa khráp_) – It's hot today, wearing shorts is better!

---

#### 6. **หน้า** (_nâa_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Face / Page / Next / Front.
2. **Meaning**: Page (in a book), next (future time/month), or physical face.
3. **Word Breakdown**:
    - **หน้า** (_nâa_)
    - _Phonetic_: _nâa_ (Falling tone, Special High Consonant **หน** + long vowel **-า** + Mai Tho **-้**)
4. **English Meaning, Cultural & Historical Context**: Polysemous Thai word serving as a noun (_face_), classifier/location (_page/front_), and time modifier (_next_).
5. **Modern Daily Usage**: Extremely common in classrooms (**หน้า 48**), scheduling (**เดือนหน้า** _next month_), and spatial directions (**ข้างหน้า** _in front_).
6. **Formality**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **เปิดหนังสือหน้าสี่สิบแปดครับ** (_pə̀ət naŋ-sUu nâa sìi sìp pɛ̀ɛt khráp_) – Open the book to page forty-eight.
8. **Synonyms**: **ใบหน้า** (_bay-nâa_ – physical face).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **เจอกันเดือนหน้านะครับ** (_cəə kan dUan nâa ná khráp_) – See you next month!

---

#### 7. **ห้อง** (_hông_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Room / Chamber.
2. **Meaning**: Room or indoor room facility.
3. **Word Breakdown**:
    - **ห้อง** (_hông_)
    - _Phonetic_: _hông_ (Falling tone, Initial High Consonant **ห** + vowel **-อ** + final **ง** + Mai Tho **-้**)
4. **English Meaning, Cultural & Historical Context**: Structural prefix attached to primary activities to name designated home or building rooms.
5. **Modern Daily Usage**: Universal room prefix used daily (**ห้องน้ำ** _restroom_, **ห้องเรียน** _classroom_, **ห้องครัว** _kitchen_, **ห้องนอน** _bedroom_).
6. **Formality**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **ห้องครัวอยู่ที่ไหนครับ** (_hông khrua yùu thîi năy khráp_) – Where is the kitchen?
8. **Synonyms**: **ห้องหับ** (_hông-hàp_ – traditional room).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **ขอไปห้องน้ำหน่อยครับ** (_khɔ̌ɔ pay hông-náam nɔ̀oy khráp_) – May I go to the restroom, please?

---

#### 8. **ไข่** (_khày_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Egg.
2. **Meaning**: Egg (specifically chicken egg **ไข่ไก่**).
3. **Word Breakdown**:
    - **ไข่** (_khày_)
    - _Phonetic_: _khày_ (Low tone, Initial High Consonant **ข** + special vowel **ไ-** + Mai Ek **-่**)
4. **English Meaning, Cultural & Historical Context**: The ultimate Thai culinary comfort topping. A fried egg (**ไข่ดาว**) or Thai omelet (**ไข่เจียว**) is added on top of almost any stir-fried dish or curry.
5. **Modern Usage**: Used daily when buying groceries or customizing restaurant orders.
6. **Formality**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **ขอข้าวผัดกะเพราไก่ไข่ดาวจานหนึ่งครับ** (_khɔ̌ɔ khâaw phàt ka-phraw kày khày daaw caan nUuŋ khráp_) – May I have chicken basil fried rice with a fried egg, please!
8. **Synonyms**: **ไข่ไก่** (_khày kày_ – chicken egg specifically).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **ขอเพิ่มไข่ดาวด้วยครับ** (_khɔ̌ɔ phə̂əm khày daaw dûay khráp_) – Please add a fried egg as well!

Page 49 of _**Aan Khian Thai 2.pdf**_ (Lesson 8: Initial High Consonants and Tone Marks / **บทที่ ๘**) teaches **Section II: Dead Syllables (Exercise 3: Practice Reading Aloud and Writing Phonetic Symbols)**.

In Thai tone rules, when an **Initial High Consonant** (ข, ฉ, ถ, ผ, ฝ, ส, ห, or Leading ห clusters like หน, หม, หล, หย) is paired with a **Dead Syllable** (a short vowel or a syllable ending in a stop consonant **-ก**, **-ด**, **-บ**), it automatically produces a **Low tone** (เสียงเอก) without requiring any tone mark.

Page 49 presents 10 target high-consonant dead syllable vocabulary words for reading and writing practice:

---

### 1. **ผัก** (_phàk_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Vegetable.
2. **Meaning**: Vegetables / Fresh greens.
3. **Word Breakdown**: **ผัก** (_phàk_ – Low tone: Initial High Consonant **ผ** + short vowel **-ะ** + final stop **ก**).
4. **English Meaning, Cultural & Historical Context**: Vegetables are a foundational component of Thai cuisine, eaten raw alongside spicy dipping pastes (**น้ำพริก**), stir-fried in woks, or cooked in herbal soups.
5. **Modern Daily Usage**: Used daily when shopping at fresh markets (**ตลาดสด**) or ordering food (**ผัดผัก** = stir-fried vegetables).
6. **Formality Level**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **ผมชอบทานผักสดครับ** (_phŏm chɔ̂ɔp thaan phàk sòt khráp_) – "I like eating fresh vegetables."
8. **Synonyms**: **พืชผัก** (_phUut-phàk_ – formal crops/vegetables).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **ขอเพิ่มผักสดด้วยครับ** (_khɔ̌ɔ phə̂əm phàk sòt dûay khráp_) – "Please add more fresh vegetables as well!"

---

### 2. **สด** (_sòt_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Fresh.
2. **Meaning**: Fresh, raw, or live (e.g., fresh produce, seafood, flowers, or fresh air).
3. **Word Breakdown**: **สด** (_sòt_ – Low tone: Initial High Consonant **ส** + unwritten short vowel **-โอะ** + final stop **ด**).
4. **English Meaning, Cultural & Historical Context**: Essential quality descriptor in Thai fresh markets and coastal dining (**กุ้งสด** = fresh shrimp).
5. **Modern Daily Usage**: Used constantly when selecting food ingredients or praising the quality of market goods.
6. **Formality Level**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **ผักและผลไม้ที่นี่สดมากครับ** (_phàk lɛ́ phŏn-la-máay thîi-nîi sòt mâak khráp_) – "The vegetables and fruit here are very fresh."
8. **Synonyms**: **สดใหม่** (_sòt-mày_ – fresh and new).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **อาหารทะเลที่นี่สดมากครับ** (_?aa-hăan tha-lee thîi-nîi sòt mâak khráp_) – "The seafood here is very fresh!"

---

### 3. **หนัก** (_nàk_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Heavy / Severe.
2. **Meaning**: "Heavy" in physical weight, or "severe/intense" (such as hard work or heavy rain).
3. **Word Breakdown**: **หนัก** (_nàk_ – Low tone: Special High Consonant cluster **หน** + short vowel **-ะ** + final stop **ก**).
4. **English Meaning, Cultural & Historical Context**: Used both for physical mass and metaphorically to describe intensive effort (**ทำงานหนัก** = work hard).
5. **Modern Daily Usage**: Used daily when describing luggage, heavy tasks, or heavy weather.
6. **Formality Level**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **กระเป๋าใบนี้หนักมากครับ** (_kra-păw bay níi nàk mâak khráp_) – "This bag is very heavy."
8. **Synonyms**: **หนักอึ้ง** (_nàk-Ûŋ_ – extremely heavy).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **กระเป๋าหนักไหมครับ ผมช่วยถือได้นะ** (_kra-păw nàk máy khráp, phŏm chûay thUu dâay ná_) – "Is the bag heavy? I can help carry it!"

---

### 4. **หก** (_hòk_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Six / Spill.
2. **Meaning**: The number "six" (6), or the action verb "to spill / slosh out" (liquids).
3. **Word Breakdown**: **หก** (_hòk_ – Low tone: Initial High Consonant **ห** + unwritten short vowel **-โอะ** + final stop **ก**).
4. **English Meaning, Cultural & Historical Context**: Serves as the number 6 in counting, and also describes accidental liquid spills (**น้ำหก**).
5. **Modern Daily Usage**: Used daily for time-telling (**หกโมง** = 6 o'clock) and daily accidental spills.
6. **Formality Level**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **เราจะเจอกันตอนหกโมงเย็นครับ** (_raw ca cəə kan toon hòk mooŋ yen khráp_) – "We will meet at 6:00 PM."
8. **Synonyms**: N/A (specific number / action verb).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **เจอกันตอนหกโมงเย็นนะครับ** (_cəə kan toon hòk mooŋ yen ná khráp_) – "See you at 6:00 PM!"

---

### 5. **หมด** (_mòt_)

1. **Literal Translation**: All gone / Finished / Completely.
2. **Meaning**: "All gone", "run out", "finished", or "completely/entirely".
3. **Word Breakdown**: **หมด** (_mòt_ – Low tone: Special High Consonant cluster **หม** + unwritten short vowel **-โอะ** + final stop **ด**).
4. **English Meaning, Cultural & Historical Context**: Core daily Thai word indicating that supplies, food, money, or stock have been depleted.
5. **Modern Daily Usage**: Used daily when items or dishes sell out (**ขายหมด** = sold out, **เงินหมด** = out of money).
6. **Formality Level**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **ข้าวมันไก่ร้านนี้ขายหมดแล้วครับ** (_khâaw-man-kày ráan níi khăay mòt lɛ́ɛw khráp_) – "Chicken rice at this shop is already sold out."
8. **Synonyms**: **เกลี้ยง** (_klîaŋ_ – completely cleared/gone).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **เมนูนี้หมดแล้วครับ** (_mee-nuu níi mòt lɛ́ɛw khráp_) – "This menu item is sold out!"

---

### 6. **หยด** (_yòt_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Drop / Drip.
2. **Meaning**: A single "drop" (noun/classifier), or the action "to drip" (verb).
3. **Word Breakdown**: **หยด** (_yòt_ – Low tone: Special High Consonant cluster **หย** + unwritten short vowel **-โอะ** + final stop **ด**).
4. **English Meaning, Cultural & Historical Context**: Used for drops of water, rain, medicine, or sauces.
5. **Modern Daily Usage**: Used when discussing leaky faucets (**น้ำหยด**) or applying eye/ear drops.
6. **Formality Level**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **น้ำหยดจากหลังคาครับ** (_náam yòt càak lăŋ-khaa khráp_) – "Water is dripping from the roof."
8. **Synonyms**: **หยดน้ำ** (_yòt-náam_ – water drop).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **ก๊อกน้ำมีน้ำหยดครับ** (_kɔ́ɔk-náam mii náam yòt khráp_) – "The water faucet is dripping."

---

### 7. **ฝึก** (_fùk_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Practice / Train / Exercise.
2. **Meaning**: "To practice", "train", or "exercise a skill".
3. **Word Breakdown**: **ฝึก** (_fùk_ – Low tone: Initial High Consonant **ฝ** + short vowel **-ึ** + final stop **ก**).
4. **English Meaning, Cultural & Historical Context**: Essential educational verb used when acquiring languages (**ฝึกพูด** = practice speaking, **ฝึกอ่าน** = practice reading) or sports.
5. **Modern Daily Usage**: Used daily by students, language learners, and athletes.
6. **Formality Level**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **ผมต้องฝึกพูดภาษาไทยทุกวันครับ** (_phŏm tɔ̂ɔŋ fùk phûat phaa-săa thay thúk wan khráp_) – "I must practice speaking Thai every day."
8. **Synonyms**: **ฝึกหัด** (_fùk-hàt_ – practice/train), **ซ้อม** (_sɔ́ɔm_ – rehearse/practice).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **ผมกำลังฝึกพูดภาษาไทยครับ** (_phŏm kam-laŋ fùk phûat phaa-săa thay khráp_) – "I am currently practicing speaking Thai."

### 8. **ผัด** (_phàt_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Stir-fry / Postpone.
2. **Meaning**: "To stir-fry" (cooking verb), "a stir-fried dish", or "to postpone/delay".
3. **Word Breakdown**: **ผัด** (_phàt_ – Low tone: Initial High Consonant **ผ** + short vowel **-ะ** + final stop **ด**).
4. **English Meaning, Cultural & Historical Context**: High-heat stir-frying in a wok is one of Thailand's defining culinary techniques, featured in dishes like **ผัดไทย** (_Pad Thai_) and **ผัดกะเพรา** (_Pad Kaprow_).
5. **Modern Daily Usage**: Used multiple times every day when ordering meals at street stalls and food courts.
6. **Formality Level**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **ผมชอบทานผัดผักรวมมิตรครับ** (_phŏm chɔ̂ɔp thaan phàt phàk ruam-mít khráp_) – "I like eating stir-fried mixed vegetables."
8. **Synonyms**: N/A (specific cooking style).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **ขอผัดไทยกุ้งจานหนึ่งครับ** (_khɔ̌ɔ phàt thay kúŋ caan nUuŋ khráp_) – "May I have one plate of shrimp Pad Thai, please."

---

### 9. **ผิด** (_phìt_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Wrong / Mistake / Incorrect.
2. **Meaning**: "Wrong", "incorrect", "mistaken", or "fault/error".
3. **Word Breakdown**: **ผิด** (_phìt_ – Low tone: Initial High Consonant **ผ** + short vowel **-ิ** + final stop **ด**).
4. **English Meaning, Cultural & Historical Context**: Crucial polite word when acknowledging or correcting mistakes (**ขอโทษครับ ผมพูดผิด** = "Sorry, I misspoke").
5. **Modern Daily Usage**: Essential in daily polite conversation when correcting errors or clarifying misunderstandings.
6. **Formality Level**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **ขอโทษครับ ผมคิดว่าผมเข้าใจผิด** (_khɔ̌ɔ-thôot khráp, phŏm khít wâa phŏm khâw-cay phìt_) – "I'm sorry, I think I misunderstood."
8. **Synonyms**: **ผิดพลาด** (_phìt-phlâat_ – make a mistake), **ไม่ถูกต้อง** (_mây thûok-tɔ̂ɔŋ_ – incorrect).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **ขอโทษครับ ผมพูดผิดครับ** (_khɔ̌ɔ-thôot khráp, phŏm phûat phìt khráp_) – "I'm sorry, I misspoke!"

---

### 10. **หลัก** (_làk_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Main / Principle / Post / Pillar.
2. **Meaning**: "Main", "primary", "principle", "rule", or "post/pillar".

Page 33 of _**Phuut Thai 2**_ (Lesson 2, Part A: Grocery Shopping) teaches four key sentence structures (**โครงสร้างประโยค**) used for describing routines, conditions, additions, and unit quantities:

1. **ปกติ** (_pòk-ka-ti_ = "Normally / Usually")
2. **ถ้า** (_thâa_ = "If")
3. **ด้วย** (_dûay_ = "Also / As well / Too")
4. **อย่างละ** (_yàaŋ la_ = "[Amount] each / Per type")

---

### Step-by-Step Grammar & Word Breakdown

---

#### 1. **ปกติ** (_pòk-ka-ti_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Normal / Usual.
2. **Meaning**: "Normally", "usually", or "as a rule". Placed at the start of a sentence or before a verb to describe default habits and routines.
3. **Word Breakdown**: **ปกติ** (_pòk-ka-ti_ – Low + Low + Low tone).
4. **English Meaning, Cultural & Historical Context**: Derived from Sanskrit/Pali (_prakṛti_ meaning natural state/nature). Used to describe everyday habits or standard schedules.
5. **Modern Usage**: Used daily when discussing work hours, commute routines, or meal habits.
6. **Formality**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **ปกติผมไปทำงานแปดโมงเช้าครับ** (_pòk-ka-ti phǒm pay tham-ŋaan pɛ̀ɛt mooŋ cháaw khráp_) – "Normally I go to work at 8:00 AM."
8. **Synonyms**: **โดยทั่วไป** (_dooy thûa-pay_ – generally), **ตามปกติ** (_taam pòk-ka-ti_ – as usual).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **ปกติคุณทานข้าวเช้าที่บ้านหรือที่โรงเรียนครับ** (_pòk-ka-ti khun thaan khâaw cháaw thîi báan rú thîi roogrian khráp_) – "Normally, do you eat breakfast at home or at school?"

---

#### 2. **ถ้า** (_thâa_)

1. **Literal Translation**: If.
2. **Meaning**: Conditional conjunction "if", used to introduce conditions, hypothetical scenarios, or polite requests.
3. **Word Breakdown**: **ถ้า** (_thâa_ – Falling tone).
4. **English Meaning, Cultural & Historical Context**: Foundational conditional word in Thai grammar, frequently paired with **ก็** (_kɔ̂ɔ_ = then) in the main clause.
5. **Modern Usage**: Used constantly in daily conversation when discussing weather plans, scheduling conditions (**ถ้าว่าง** _if free_), or seeking clarification (**ถ้าไม่เข้าใจ** _if you don't understand_).
6. **Formality**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **ถ้าพรุ่งนี้ว่าง เราไปเที่ยวกันนะครับ** (_thâa phrùŋ-níi wâaŋ, raw pay thǐaw kan ná khráp_) – "If you are free tomorrow, let's go out together!"
8. **Synonyms**: **หาก** (_hàak_ – formal "if").
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **ถ้าไม่เข้าใจ ต้องถามนะครับ** (_thâa mây khâw-cay, tɔ́ŋ thǎam ná khráp_) – "If you don't understand, you must ask, okay?"

#### 3. **ด้วย** (_dûay_)

1. **Literal Translation**: With / Too / Also / As well.
2. **Meaning**: "Also", "too", "as well", or placed at the end of polite requests to add "as well" / "please".
3. **Word Breakdown**: **ด้วย** (_dûay_ – Falling tone).
4. **English Meaning, Cultural & Historical Context**: Placed at the end of a clause to indicate an addition to a list, or to soften a polite request (**ช่วย...ด้วย** = please help do ... as well).
5. **Modern Usage**: Used dozens of times daily when ordering food/drinks (**ใส่นมด้วย** _add milk as well_), buying extra items, or making requests.
6. **Formality**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **ขอชาเย็นและใส่นมด้วยครับ** (_khɔ̌ɔ chaa yen lɛ́ sày nom dûay khráp_) – "May I have iced tea, and add milk as well, please."
8. **Synonyms**: **เหมือนกัน** (_mǔan-kan_ – also/likewise), **เช่นกัน** (_chên-kan_ – formal likewise).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **ช่วยซื้อกาแฟให้หน่อย ใส่นมกับน้ำตาลด้วยนะครับ** (_chûay sÚu kaa-fɛɛ hây nɔ̀y, sày nom kàp náam-taan dûay ná khráp_) – "Please buy coffee for me, and add milk with sugar as well, okay?"

#### 4. **อย่างละ** (_yàaŋ la_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Kind/Type + per/each = Each / Per type.
2. **Meaning**: "[Amount] each" or "Each type". Used when specifying an equal quantity for two or more items listed together.
3. **Word Breakdown**: **อย่าง** (_yàaŋ_ – Low tone: classifier for kinds/types) + **ละ** (_la_ – High tone: per/each). Phonetic: _yàaŋ la_.
4. **English Meaning, Cultural & Historical Context**: Standard pattern: `[Item 1] + กับ + [Item 2] + อย่างละ + [Number] + [Classifier]`. Essential for ordering produce at fresh markets or food at restaurants.
5. **Modern Usage**: Used daily when ordering multiple items of equal quantities at market stalls, cafes, or shops.
6. **Formality**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **ขอส้มกับสับปะรดอย่างละสองกิโลครับ** (_khɔ̌ɔ sóm kàp sap-pa-rót yàaŋ la sɔ̌ɔŋ ki-loo khráp_) – "May I have two kilos each of oranges and pineapple, please."
8. **Synonyms**: **ชนิดละ** (_cha-nít la_ – formal per type).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **เจนซื้อส้มกับสับปะรดอย่างละสองกิโลครับ** (_ceen sÚu sóm kàp sap-pa-rót yàaŋ la sɔ̌ɔŋ ki-loo khráp_) – "Jane buys oranges and pineapples, two kilograms of each."
