#### 1. **รองเท้าแตะ** (_rooŋ-tháaw tɛ̀_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Shoe + slap/tap = Flip-flops / Sandals.
2. **Meaning**: Flip-flops, sandals, or casual slippers.
3. **Word Breakdown**:
    - **รองเท้า** (_rooŋ-tháaw_ – High + High tone): Shoes / Footwear.
    - **แตะ** (_tɛ̀_ – Low tone): Tap / Touch lightly (imitating the slapping sound on the heel).
4. **English Meaning & Cultural Context**: Due to Thailand's tropical climate, flip-flops are the most common everyday footwear for casual outings and beach trips. In Thai etiquette, shoes or sandals are always removed before entering homes or temple buildings.
5. **Modern Usage**: Used daily when discussing casual footwear or packing for travel.
6. **Formality Level**: Casual to Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **ผมจะเอารองเท้าแตะไปกรุงเทพฯ ครับ** (_phŏm ca ?aw rooŋ-tháaw tɛ̀ pay kruŋ-thêep khráp_) – "I will bring flip-flops to Bangkok."
8. **Synonyms**: **รองเท้าสวม** (_rooŋ-tháaw sùam_ – slip-on shoes).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **เอารองเท้าแตะไปด้วยนะครับ** (_?aw rooŋ-tháaw tɛ̀ pay dûay ná khráp_) – "Bring flip-flops along as well, okay?"

4. **English Meaning & Cultural Context**: A practical clothing choice for hot weather in Thailand. While popular for casual leisure, shorts are considered inappropriate attire when visiting temples (_วัด_), royal palaces, or government offices.
5. **Modern Usage**: Used daily when packing for trips or buying leisure wear.
6. **Formality Level**: Casual to Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **เอากางเกงขาสั้นไปสองตัวครับ** (_?aw kaaŋ-keeŋ khăa sân pay sɔ̌ɔŋ tua khráp_) – "Bring two pairs of shorts."

9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **ขอกางเกงขาสั้นสีดำตัวหนึ่งครับ** (_khɔ̌ɔ kaaŋ-keeŋ khăa sân sĭi dam tua nUuŋ khráp_) – "May I have one pair of black shorts, please."

Here is the detailed step-by-step breakdown of the target vocabulary and sentence patterns taught on Page 40:

---

### 1. **หลวมไป** (_lŭam pay_) — Too loose

1. **Literal Translation**: Loose + excessively (go) = Too loose.
2. **Meaning**: Describing clothing that is too loose, baggy, or oversized for a wearer.
3. **Word Breakdown**:
    - **หลวม** (_lŭam_ – Rising tone): Loose / Baggy.
    - **ไป** (_pay_ – Mid tone): Excessively / Too (when following an adjective).
4. **English Meaning, Cultural & Historical Context**: Attaching **ไป** after an adjective indicates an excess degree beyond an ideal fit. In Thai retail markets, expressing fit issues like **หลวมไป** helps vendors select better-fitting alternatives.
5. **Modern Daily Usage**: Used constantly when fitting clothes in fitting rooms or at market stalls.
6. **Formality Level**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **เสื้อตัวนี้หลวมไปหน่อยครับ** (_sÛa tua níi lŭam pay nɔ̀oy khráp_) – "This shirt is a bit too loose."
8. **Synonyms**: **หลวมเกินไป** (_lŭam kəən-pay_ – formal excessively loose).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **เสื้อตัวนี้หลวมไป มีขนาดเล็กกว่านี้ไหมครับ** (_sÛa tua níi lŭam pay, mii kha-nàat lék kwàa níi máy khráp_)

---

### 2. **...กว่านี้** (_...kwàa níi_) — ...than this / -er than this

1. **Literal Translation**: More than + this = "-er than this" / "More... than this".
2. **Meaning**: Comparative pattern placed after an adjective to request an item that is "more [adjective] than this" (e.g., smaller, larger, cheaper).
3. **Word Breakdown**:
    - **กว่า** (_kwàa_ – Low tone): More than / -er (comparative marker).
    - **นี้** (_níi_ – High tone): This.
4. **English Meaning, Cultural & Historical Context**: Essential comparative structure (`Adjective + กว่านี้`) used while holding or trying an item to ask for an alternative size or option.
5. **Modern Daily Usage**: Used daily while shopping (**เล็กกว่านี้** = smaller than this, **ใหญ่กว่านี้** = larger than this, **ถูกกว่านี้** = cheaper than this).
6. **Formality Level**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **มีขนาดใหญ่กว่านี้ไหมครับ** (_mii kha-nàat yày kwàa níi máy khráp_) – "Do you have a size larger than this?"
8. **Synonyms**: **กว่านี้อีก** (_kwàa níi ?ìik_ – even more than this).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **มีตัวเล็กกว่านี้ไหมครับ** (_mii tua lék kwàa níi máy khráp_)

---

### 3. **ลอง** (_looŋ_) — To try / To try on

1. **Literal Translation**: Try / Test.
2. **Meaning**: "To try" or "to try on" clothing, shoes, or sizes.
3. **Word Breakdown**:
    - **ลอง** (_looŋ_ – Mid tone): Try / Test.
4. **English Meaning, Cultural & Historical Context**: Thai sales attendants frequently encourage shoppers by saying **ลองได้นะคะ** (_you can try it on_).
5. **Modern Daily Usage**: Placed before clothing nouns or sizes (**ลองเสื้อ** _try on a shirt_, **ลองไซส์** _try a size_).
6. **Formality Level**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **ผมขอลองเสื้อตัวนี้ได้ไหมครับ** (_phŏm khɔ̌ɔ looŋ sÛa tua níi dâay máy khráp_) – "May I try on this shirt, please?"
8. **Synonyms**: **ลองดู** (_looŋ-duu_ – casual try and see).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **ขอลองตัวเล็กกว่านี้หน่อยครับ** (_khɔ̌ɔ looŋ tua lék kwàa níi nɔ̀oy khráp_)

---

### 4. **ใส่สบาย** (_sày sa-baay_) — Comfortable to wear

1. **Literal Translation**: Wear + comfortable = Comfortable to wear.
2. **Meaning**: Clothing that is pleasant, soft, or comfortable to wear.
3. **Word Breakdown**:
    - **ใส่** (_sày_ – Low tone): Wear / Put on.
    - **สบาย** (_sa-baay_ – Low + Mid tone): Comfortable / Pleasant.
4. **English Meaning, Cultural & Historical Context**: **สบาย** (_sabai_) is a core Thai cultural concept denoting physical ease and relaxed comfort. Reduplicating it (**ใส่สบายๆ**) emphasizes a casual, cozy feel.
5. **Modern Daily Usage**: Used daily when complimenting garments or describing preferred clothing styles.
6. **Formality Level**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **ผมชอบเสื้อผ้าที่ใส่สบายๆ ครับ** (_phŏm chɔ̂ɔp sÛa-phâa thîi sày sa-baay sa-baay khráp_) – "I like clothes that are comfortable to wear."
8. **Synonyms**: **สวมใส่สบาย** (_sûam-sày sa-baay_ – formal).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **อยากได้เสื้อตัวหลวมๆ ใส่สบายๆ สักตัวครับ** (_yàak dâay sÛa tua lŭam-lŭam sày sa-baay sa-baay sák tua khráp_)

---

### 5. **เสื้อยืด** (_sÛa yUut_) — T-shirt

1. **Literal Translation**: Shirt + stretch/expand = T-shirt.
2. **Meaning**: T-shirt.
3. **Word Breakdown**:
    - **เสื้อ** (_sÛa_ – Falling tone): Shirt / Top.
    - **ยืด** (_yUut_ – Falling tone): Stretchable / Elastic.
4. **English Meaning, Cultural & Historical Context**: Compound word describing soft cotton knit tops, which are the quintessential everyday casual wear across Thailand's warm climate.
5. **Modern Daily Usage**: Used constantly when buying casual tops at markets and shopping malls.
6. **Formality Level**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **เสื้อยืดตัวนี้ราคาเท่าไหร่ครับ** (_sÛa yUut tua níi raa-khaa thâw-ray khráp_) – "How much is this T-shirt?"
8. **Synonyms**: N/A.
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **เสื้อยืดตัวนี้มีเบอร์เล็กกว่านี้ไหมครับ** (_sÛa yUut tua níi mii bəə lék kwàa níi máy khráp_)

---

### 6. **ขนาด / เบอร์** (_kha-nàat / bəə_) — Size / Number

1. **Literal Translation**: Size / Number.
2. **Meaning**: Clothing/shoe size or size designation.
3. **Word Breakdown**:
    - **ขนาด** (_kha-nàat_ – Low + Low tone): Size / Dimensions (formal).
    - **เบอร์** (_bəə_ – Mid tone): Number / Size designation (casual loanword from "number").
4. **English Meaning, Cultural & Historical Context**: **ขนาด** is the formal noun for physical dimensions, whereas **เบอร์** is widely used in everyday spoken Thai when requesting specific numerical or letter sizes.
5. **Modern Daily Usage**: Used constantly when asking shopkeepers for different clothing sizes.
6. **Formality Level**: **ขนาด** (Neutral to Formal), **เบอร์** (Casual to Neutral).
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **เสื้อตัวนี้มีเบอร์อะไรบ้างครับ** (_sÛa tua níi mii bəə ?a-ray báaŋ khráp_) – "What sizes does this shirt come in?"
8. **Synonyms**: **ไซส์** (_sáy_ – casual English loanword "size").
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **มีขนาดเล็กกว่านี้ไหมครับ** (_mii kha-nàat lék kwàa níi máy khráp_)

---

### 7. **รอสักครู่** (_rɔɔ sák-khrûu_) — Please wait a moment

1. **Literal Translation**: Wait + roughly + moment = Please wait a moment.
2. **Meaning**: "Please wait a moment" / "Just a second".
3. **Word Breakdown**:
    - **รอ** (_rɔɔ_ – Mid tone): Wait.
    - **สักครู่** (_sák-khrûu_ – High + Falling tone): A moment / Shortly.
4. **English Meaning, Cultural & Historical Context**: Standard polite service response spoken by shop assistants, waiters, and hotel staff when fetching items or looking up stock.
5. **Modern Daily Usage**: Universal polite service phrase across Thai retail and hospitality.
6. **Formality Level**: Polite to Formal.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **รอสักครู่นะครับ เดี๋ยวผมไปหยิบให้** (_rɔɔ sák-khrûu ná khráp, dĭaw phŏm pay yìp hây_) – "Please wait a moment, I will go fetch it for you."
8. **Synonyms**: **รอแป๊บหนึ่ง** (_rɔɔ pɛ́p nUuŋ_ – casual wait a second).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **มีค่ะ รอสักครู่นะคะ** (_mii khâ, rɔɔ sák-khrûu ná khâ_)

Here is the step-by-step breakdown of each line from the page 41 dialogue following your 10-part learning format:

---

### Line 1: **นิดาซื้ออะไรบ้างจ้ะ** (_ní-daa sÚu ?a-ray báaŋ cá_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Nida + buy + what + [plural inquiry particle] + [female casual particle].
2. **Meaning**: "Nida, what items did you buy?"
3. **Word Breakdown & Pronunciation**:
    - **นิดา** (_ní-daa_): Proper name Nida.
    - **ซื้อ** (_sÚu_ – High tone): Buy / Purchase.
    - **อะไร** (_?a-ray_ – Mid + Mid tone): What.
    - **บ้าง** (_báaŋ_ – Falling tone): Plural inquiry particle asking for a list of items.
    - **จ้ะ** (_cá_ – High tone): Casual female polite particle.
4. **English Meaning, Cultural & Historical Context**: **บ้าง** (_báaŋ_) is attached to **อะไร** (_what_) when expecting a list of multiple items rather than a single thing. **จ้ะ** (_cá_) adds an informal, friendly tone among female peers.
5. **Modern Daily Usage**: Used daily when asking friends or shopping partners what they purchased.
6. **Formality Level**: Casual to Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **คุณซื้ออะไรมาจากตลาดบ้างครับ** (_khun sÚu ?a-ray maa càak ta-làat báaŋ khráp_) – What items did you buy from the market?
8. **Synonyms**: **ซื้ออะไรมาบ้าง** (_sÚu ?a-ray maa báaŋ_ – what did you buy back?).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **คุณซื้ออะไรมาบ้างครับ** (_khun sÚu ?a-ray maa báaŋ khráp_).

---

### Line 2: **ซื้อเสื้อยืด ๒ ตัว กับกางเกงขาสั้นตัวหนึ่ง แล้วแอนนาล่ะ** (_sÚu sÛa yUut sɔ̌ɔŋ tua kàp kaaŋ-keeŋ khăa sân tua nUuŋ, lɛ́ɛw ?an-naa lâ_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Buy + T-shirt + 2 + [classifier] + with + shorts + [classifier] + one, + then + Anna + [question particle].
2. **Meaning**: "I bought 2 T-shirts and a pair of shorts. What about you, Anna?"
3. **Word Breakdown & Pronunciation**:
    - **ซื้อ** (_sÚu_ – High tone): Buy / Purchase.
    - **เสื้อยืด** (_sÛa yUut_ – Falling + Falling tone): T-shirt.
    - **สอง** (_sɔ̌ɔŋ_ – Rising tone): Two (2).
    - **ตัว** (_tua_ – Mid tone): Classifier for clothing items, tops, pants, and skirts.
    - **กับ** (_kàp_ – Low tone): With / And.
    - **กางเกงขาสั้น** (_kaaŋ-keeŋ khăa sân_ – Mid + Mid + Rising + Falling tone): Shorts / Short pants.
    - **ตัวหนึ่ง** (_tua nUuŋ_ – Mid + Low tone): One [item] (classifier + one).
    - **แล้ว...ล่ะ** (_lɛ́ɛw ... lâ_ – High + Falling tone): "And how about...?" / "What about...?".
4. **English Meaning, Cultural & Historical Context**: **ตัว** (_tua_) is the standard numerical classifier for garments. Placing **ตัวหนึ่ง** (_one item_) after a noun is the natural spoken way to say "one / a [garment]". **แล้ว...ล่ะ** returns a question smoothly to your partner.
5. **Modern Daily Usage**: Used daily when summarizing shopping items and asking return questions.
6. **Formality Level**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **ผมซื้อเสื้อยืดสองตัวครับ แล้วคุณล่ะครับ** (_phŏm sÚu sÛa yUut sɔ̌ɔŋ tua khráp, lɛ́ɛw khun lâ khráp_) – I bought two T-shirts. How about you?
8. **Synonyms**: **และคุณล่ะ** (_lɛ́ khun lâ_ – and you?).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **ผมซื้อเสื้อยืดสองตัวกับกางเกงขาสั้นหนึ่งตัวครับ แล้วคุณล่ะครับ** (_phŏm sÚu sÛa yUut sɔ̌ɔŋ tua kàp kaaŋ-keeŋ khăa sân nUuŋ tua khráp, lɛ́ɛw khun lâ khráp_).

---

### Line 3: **ซื้อเสื้อเชิ้ต ๓ ตัว กระโปรง ๒ ตัวจ้ะ** (_sÚu sÛa chə́ət săam tua kra-prooŋ sɔ̌ɔŋ tua cá_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Buy + dress shirt + 3 + [classifier] + skirt + 2 + [classifier] + [female casual particle].
2. **Meaning**: "I bought 3 dress shirts and 2 skirts!"
3. **Word Breakdown & Pronunciation**:
    - **ซื้อ** (_sÚu_ – High tone): Buy / Purchase.
    - **เสื้อเชิ้ต** (_sÛa chə́ət_ – Falling + High tone): Button-down / Collared dress shirt (English loanword).
    - **สาม** (_săam_ – Rising tone): Three (3).
    - **ตัว** (_tua_ – Mid tone): Garment classifier.
    - **กระโปรง** (_kra-prooŋ_ – Low + Mid tone): Skirt.
    - **สอง** (_sɔ̌ɔŋ_ – Rising tone): Two (2).
    - **จ้ะ** (_cá_ – High tone): Casual female statement particle.
4. **English Meaning, Cultural & Historical Context**: **เสื้อเชิ้ต** is borrowed from the English word "shirt" to specify button-down collared tops, distinguishing them from casual **เสื้อยืด** (_T-shirts_). **กระโปรง** refers to skirts.
5. **Modern Daily Usage**: Used daily when discussing wardrobe items or clothing shopping.
6. **Formality Level**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **ผมอยากได้เสื้อเชิ้ตสีขาวสองตัวครับ** (_phŏm yàak dâay sÛa chə́ət sĭi khăaw sɔ̌ɔŋ tua khráp_) – I would like to get two white dress shirts.
8. **Synonyms**: **เสื้อทำงาน** (_sÛa tham-ŋaan_ – work shirt).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **ผมซื้อเสื้อเชิ้ตสามตัวและกระโปรงสองตัวครับ** (_phŏm sÚu sÛa chə́ət săam tua lɛ́ kra-prooŋ sɔ̌ɔŋ tua khráp_).

---

### Line 4: **กระโปรงน่ารักจัง** (_kra-prooŋ náa-ràk caŋ_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Skirt + cute/lovely + so/very.
2. **Meaning**: "The skirts are so cute!"
3. **Word Breakdown & Pronunciation**:
    - **กระโปรง** (_kra-prooŋ_ – Low + Mid tone): Skirt.
    - **น่ารัก** (_náa-ràk_ – Falling + High tone): Cute / Lovely / Adorable (**น่า** _inviting_ + **รัก** _love_).
    - **จัง** (_caŋ_ – Mid tone): So / Very (expressive particle).
4. **English Meaning, Cultural & Historical Context**: **น่ารัก** literally means "inviting love". **จัง** is an enthusiastic modifier placed after adjectives in spoken Thai to express strong feeling.
5. **Modern Daily Usage**: Used daily when complimenting clothes, pets, children, or nice gestures.
6. **Formality Level**: Casual to Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **เสื้อตัวนี้น่ารักจังครับ** (_sÛa tua níi náa-ràk caŋ khráp_) – This shirt is so cute!
8. **Synonyms**: **น่ารักมาก** (_náa-ràk mâak_ – very cute).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **กระโปรงน่ารักมากครับ** (_kra-prooŋ náa-ràk mâak khráp_).

### Line 5: **ขอบใจจ้ะ** (_khɔ̂ɔp-cay cá_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Thank + heart + [female casual particle].
2. **Meaning**: "Thank you!" (informal / friendly).
3. **Word Breakdown & Pronunciation**:
    - **ขอบใจ** (_khɔ̂ɔp-cay_ – Falling + Mid tone): Thank you (casual/informal).
    - **จ้ะ** (_cá_ – High tone): Casual female polite particle.
4. **English Meaning, Cultural & Historical Context**: **ขอบใจ** (_khɔ̂ɔp-cay_) is used strictly between close peers of equal age/status, or by an elder addressing a younger person. When thanking seniors, strangers, or teachers, always use **ขอบคุณ** (_khɔ̂ɔp-khun_) instead.
5. **Modern Daily Usage**: Used daily among close friends, classmates, or when addressing younger family members.
6. **Formality Level**: Casual / Informal (do NOT use with elders or teachers!).
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **ขอบใจมากนะเพื่อน** (_khɔ̂ɔp-cay mâak ná phûan_) – Thanks a lot, my friend!
8. **Synonyms**: **ขอบคุณ** (_khɔ̂ɔp-khun_ – polite/formal thank you).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **ขอบคุณมากครับ** (_khɔ̂ɔp-khun mâak khráp_).

### **Step-by-Step Breakdown of Key Vocabulary Taught on Page 42**

---

#### 1. **เสื้อผ้า** (_sÛa-phâa_) – Clothes / Clothing

1. **Literal Translation**: Shirt + cloth = **Clothes / Clothing**.
2. **Overall Meaning**: Apparel, clothes, or garments in general.
3. **Word Breakdown with Phonetic Pronunciation**:
    - **เสื้อ** (_sÛa_ – Falling tone): Shirt / Top.
    - **ผ้า** (_phâa_ – Falling tone): Cloth / Fabric.
4. **Context & English Meaning**: Compound noun uniting **เสื้อ** (_shirt_) and **ผ้า** (_cloth/fabric_) to refer to clothing as a whole.
5. **Modern Daily Usage**: Used daily when talking about shopping for clothes (**ซื้อเสื้อผ้า**), laundry, or choosing outfits.
6. **Formality Level**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **วันนี้ผมจะไปซื้อเสื้อผ้าที่ห้างครับ** (_wan-níi phŏm ca pay sÚu sÛa-phâa thîi hâaŋ khráp_) – "Today I am going to buy clothes at the mall."
8. **Synonyms**: **เครื่องแต่งกาย** (_khrÛaŋ tɛ̀ɛŋ-kaay_ – formal attire).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **แอนนาซื้อเสื้อผ้าหลายอย่างครับ** (_?an-naa sÚu sÛa-phâa lăay yàaŋ khráp_)

---

#### 2. **อ้วนขึ้น** (_?ûan khÛn_) – Gained Weight

1. **Literal Translation**: Fat/Chubby + go up = **Gained weight**.
2. **Overall Meaning**: To put on weight or gain weight.
3. **Word Breakdown with Phonetic Pronunciation**:
    - **อ้วน** (_?ûan_ – Falling tone): Fat / Chubby.
    - **ขึ้น** (_khÛn_ – Falling tone): Up / Increase / Rise.
4. **Context & English Meaning**: **ขึ้น** attached after body condition adjectives indicates an upward change (**อ้วนขึ้น** = gained weight, **สูงขึ้น** = grew taller).
5. **Modern Daily Usage**: Commonly spoken among friends when explaining why clothing fit has changed.
6. **Formality Level**: Casual to Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **ช่วงนี้ผมอ้วนขึ้น เสื้อเลยแน่นครับ** (_chûaŋ níi phŏm ?ûan khÛn, sÛa ləəy nɛ̂n khráp_) – "Lately I've gained weight, so my shirt feels tight."
8. **Synonyms**: **น้ำหนักเพิ่มขึ้น** (_náam-hnàk phə̂əm khÛn_ – formal weight increased).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **แอนนาอ้วนขึ้นนิดหน่อยครับ** (_?an-naa ?ûan khÛn nít-nɔ̀oy khráp_)

---

#### 3. **ตัวเล็กกว่านี้** (_tua lék kwàa níi_) – Smaller size than this

1. **Literal Translation**: Body/Item + small + more than + this = **Smaller item/size than this**.
2. **Overall Meaning**: A size or item that is smaller than the current one being held or tried on.
3. **Word Breakdown with Phonetic Pronunciation**:
    - **ตัว** (_tua_ – Mid tone): Garment/item classifier.
    - **เล็ก** (_lék_ – High tone): Small.
    - **กว่า** (_kwàa_ – Low tone): More than / -er (comparative marker).
    - **นี้** (_níi_ – High tone): This.
4. **Context & English Meaning**: Standard comparative pattern (`Classifier + Adjective + กว่านี้`) used when asking shopkeepers for size adjustments.
5. **Modern Daily Usage**: Essential phrase used continuously when trying on clothes in stores.
6. **Formality Level**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **มีตัวเล็กกว่านี้ไหมครับ** (_mii tua lék kwàa níi máy khráp_) – "Do you have one smaller than this?"
8. **Synonyms**: **ขนาดเล็กกว่านี้** (_kha-nàat lék kwàa níi_ – formal smaller size than this).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **ขอลองตัวเล็กกว่านี้หน่อยครับ** (_khɔ̌ɔ looŋ tua lék kwàa níi nɔ̀oy khráp_)

---

#### 4. **ฟรีไซส์** (_frii sáy_) – Free Size (One-Size-Fits-All)

1. **Literal Translation**: Free + size = **Free size / One-size-fits-all**.
2. **Overall Meaning**: Clothing made in a flexible, standard one-size fit.
3. **Word Breakdown with Phonetic Pronunciation**:
    - **ฟรี** (_frii_ – Mid tone): Free (English loanword).
    - **ไซส์** (_sáy_ – High tone): Size (English loanword).
4. **Context & English Meaning**: Universal commercial loanword used across Thai retail and market stalls for stretchable or oversized garments.
5. **Modern Daily Usage**: Used daily by shop assistants and shoppers when inquiring about S/M/L availability.
6. **Formality Level**: Casual to Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **เสื้อตัวนี้เป็นขนาดฟรีไซส์ครับ** (_sÛa tua níi pen kha-nàat frii sáy khráp_) – "This shirt is free size."
8. **Synonyms**: **ขนาดมาตรฐาน** (_kha-nàat maa-tra-thăan_ – formal standard size).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **เสื้อตัวนี้มีขนาดฟรีไซส์ครับ** (_sÛa tua níi mii kha-nàat frii sáy khráp_)
