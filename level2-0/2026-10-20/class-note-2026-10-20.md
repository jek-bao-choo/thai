    - **`hây` as a preposition** (`verb + ... + ให้ + someone`): "To / for someone" (e.g., _submit the report to me_).
    - **The double `hây` structure** (`ให้ + someone + verb + ให้ + recipient`): "To ask someone to do something for someone else" (e.g., _Mom asked me to buy dinner for grandma_).

### Step-by-Step Lesson


**วันนี้แม่ให้ผมทำอาหารเย็น** (_wan-níi mɛ̂ɛ hây phŏm tham ?aa-hăan yen_).

---

#### 1. Literal Translation

**Today** + **mother** + **ask/tell** + **I (male)** + **make/cook** + **food** + **evening (dinner)**.

---

#### 2. Meaning

"Today, my mother asked me to make dinner."

---

#### 3. Word Breakdown with Phonetic Pronunciation

- **วันนี้** (_wan-níi_): Today
- **แม่** (_mɛ̂ɛ_): Mother / Mom
- **ให้** (_hây_): To ask / tell / allow / give
- **ผม** (_phŏm_): I / me (male speaker)
- **ทำ** (_tham_): To do / to make / to cook
- **อาหาร** (_?aa-hăan_): Food / meal
- **เย็น** (_yen_): Evening / cool (combines to form **อาหารเย็น** = dinner)

---

#### 4. English Meaning, Cultural, & Historical Context

- **วันนี้** (_wan-níi_): Compound of **วัน** (_day_) + **นี้** (_this_).
- **แม่** (_mɛ̂ɛ_): In Thai society, familial terms reflect warmth and social respect. Family members and close acquaintances are often addressed using kinship terms like **แม่**.
- **ให้** (_hây_): A foundational Thai word. In the pattern `ให้ + someone + verb`, it functions as a causative verb meaning to ask, instruct, or allow someone to perform an action.
- **ผม** (_phŏm_): The standard polite first-person pronoun for male speakers. Historically derived from the word for "hair of the head," expressing high respect toward the listener.
- **ทำ** (_tham_): Universal action verb for doing or creating. When paired with food (**ทำอาหาร**), it means to cook.
- **อาหารเย็น** (_?aa-hăan yen_): Compound of **อาหาร** (_food_) + **เย็น** (_evening_), literally "evening meal".

---

#### 5. Usage in Modern Daily Conversation

- **วันนี้**: Placed at the beginning or end of sentences to state plans or events occurring today.
- **แม่**: Used daily when addressing or talking about one's mother.
- **ให้**: Used constantly when telling someone to do something, giving items, or doing favors.
- **ผม**: Standard pronoun used by men when talking to teachers, colleagues, elders, or acquaintances.
- **ทำ**: Used daily for tasks, work, cooking, or hobbies.
- **อาหารเย็น**: Used when making dinner plans or talking about evening meals.

---

#### 6. Formality Level

- **วันนี้**: Neutral (used across all settings).
- **แม่**: Neutral / Affectionate (polite formal version is **คุณแม่** _khun-mɛ̂ɛ_).
- **ให้**: Neutral.
- **ผม**: Polite / Neutral (standard male speech).
- **ทำ**: Neutral.
- **อาหารเย็น**: Neutral (casual daily alternative is **ข้าวเย็น** _khâaw-yen_).

---

#### 7. Daily Example Sentences

- **วันนี้ผมมีเรียนภาษาไทยครับ** (_wan-níi phŏm mii rian phaa-săa thay khráp_) – Today I have a Thai class.
- **แม่ให้ผมไปซื้อของที่ตลาด** (_mɛ̂ɛ hây phŏm pay sÚu khɔ̌ɔŋ thîi talàat_) – Mom asked me to buy things at the market.
- **เย็นนี้เราทานอาหารเย็นด้วยกันไหมครับ** (_yen níi raw thaan ?aa-hăan yen dûay-kan máy khráp_) – Shall we have dinner together this evening?

---

#### 8. Synonyms

- **วันนี้**: **ในวันนี้** (_nay wan-níi_ – formal/written).
- **แม่**: **คุณแม่** (_khun-mɛ̂ɛ_ – polite/formal), **มารดา** (_maan-daa_ – formal/academic).
- **ให้**: **สั่งให้** (_sàŋ hây_ – command/order to), **วานให้** (_waan hây_ – ask/request nicely to).
- **ผม**: **กระผม** (_kra-phŏm_ – formal male pronoun).
- **ทำ**: **ปฏิบัติ** (_pa-ti-bàt_ – formal execute/perform).
- **อาหารเย็น**: **ข้าวเย็น** (_khâaw-yen_ – casual), **อาหารมื้อค่ำ** (_?aa-hăan mUa-khâm_ – formal).

---

#### 9. Thai Conversational Version

- **Thai Script**: **วันนี้คุณแม่ให้ผมทำอาหารเย็นครับ**
- **Phonetic**: _wan-níi khun-mɛ̂ɛ hây phŏm tham ?aa-hăan yen khráp_
- **English**: Today my mother asked me to make dinner.

#### 1. **กลัวอะไร** (_klua ?a-ray_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Afraid/scared + what.
2. **Meaning**: "What are you afraid of?"
3. **Word Breakdown**:
    - **กลัว** (_klua_): Afraid / Scared. Mid tone.
        - _4. Context_: Used when expressing fear of objects, animals, or situations.
        - _5. Daily Usage_: Common in daily conversation.
        - _6. Formality_: Neutral.
        - _7. Daily Example_: **ผมกลัวความสูง** (_phŏm klua khwaam-sŭuŋ_) – I am afraid of heights.
        - _8. Synonyms_: **เกรง** (_kreeng_ – apprehensive/formal).
    - **อะไร** (_?a-ray_): What. Mid tone (uses **สระไอ ไ-**).
        - _4. Context_: Foundational Thai question word.
        - _5. Daily Usage_: Used constantly when asking about objects, actions, or details.
        - _6. Formality_: Neutral.
        - _7. Daily Example_: **คุณชอบทานอะไรครับ** (_khun chɔ̂ɔp thaan ?a-ray khráp_) – What do you like to eat?
        - _8. Synonyms_: **สิ่งใด** (_sìŋ day_ – formal/literary).
4. **Thai Conversational Version**: **คุณกลัวอะไรครับ** (_khun klua ?a-ray khráp_) – What are you afraid of?

#### 2. **เราไปงานคนจีน** (_raw pay ŋaan khon ciin_)

    - **ไป** (_pay_): Go. Mid tone (uses **สระไอ ไ-**). Formality: Neutral.

        - _6. Formality_: Neutral.
        - _7. Daily Example_: **คืนนี้มีงานเลี้ยง** (_khUun níi mii ŋaan-líaŋ_) – There is a party tonight.

#### 4. **เรารำเป็น** (_raw ram pen_)

1. **Literal Translation**: We + traditional dance + know how to / can.
2. **Meaning**: "We know how to perform classical Thai dance."
3. **Word Breakdown**:
    - **เรา** (_raw_): We / Us (uses **สระเอา เ-า**). Formality: Neutral.

        - _5. Daily Usage_: Used when discussing Thai cultural arts and performances.
        - _6. Formality_: Neutral.
        - _7. Daily Example_: **เธอรำสวยมาก** (_thəə ram sŭay mâak_) – She dances Thai dance beautifully.

        - _4. Context_: Indicates learned skill or ability.
        - _5. Daily Usage_: Placed after action verbs (e.g. **พูดเป็น** = know how to speak).
        - _6. Formality_: Neutral.
        - _7. Daily Example_: **ผมพูดภาษาไทยเป็น** (_phŏm phûat phaa-săa thay pen_) – I know how to speak Thai.

### Item 8: ครู บอก นักเรียน เขียน อะไร

1. **Literal Translation**: Teacher + tell + student(s) + write + what.
2. **Meaning**: "What did the teacher tell the students to write?"
3. **Word Breakdown**:
    - **ครู** (_khruu_)
    - **บอก** (_bɔ̀ɔk_)
    - **นักเรียน** (_nák-rian_)
    - **เขียน** (_khĭan_)
    - **อะไร** (_?a-ray_)
4. **Context & English Meaning**:
    - **ครู** (_khruu_): Teacher / Instructor. Derived from the Sanskrit _guru_, symbolizing a deeply respected figure in Thai culture.
    - **บอก** (_bɔ̀ɔk_): To tell, instruct, or state.
    - **นักเรียน** (_nák-rian_): Student / Schoolchild (compound of **นัก** _person skilled/practicing_ + **เรียน** _study_).
    - **เขียน** (_khĭan_): To write / Compose text.
    - **อะไร** (_?a-ray_): What (uses **สระไอ ไ-**).
5. **Modern Daily Usage**: Used constantly in classroom and study contexts when asking about assignments or instructions.
6. **Formality**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **ครูบอกให้เขียนบทเรียนครับ** (_khruu bɔ̀ɔk hây khĭan bot-rian khráp_) – The teacher told us to write the lesson.
8. **Synonyms**:
    - **ครู**: **อาจารย์** (_?aa-caan_ – instructor/professor)
    - **บอก**: **กล่าว** (_klàaw_ – formal state/say)
    - **อะไร**: **สิ่งใด** (_sìŋ-day_ – formal)
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **ครูบอกให้นักเรียนเขียนอะไรครับ** (_khruu bɔ̀ɔk hây nák-rian khĭan ?a-ray khráp_)

    - **ทาน** (_thaan_): To eat (polite shortened form of **รับประทาน**).
    - **ตอนดึก** (_toon-dUuk_): Late night hours.
5. **Modern Daily Usage**: Common when asking for explanations regarding late-night eating habits or behavior.
6. **Formality**: Neutral / Polite.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **ทำไมเด็กๆ ชอบทานขนมตอนดึกครับ** (_tham-may dèk-dèk chɔ̂ɔp thaan kha-nŏm toon-dUuk khráp_) – Why do kids like eating snacks late at night?
8. **Synonyms**:
    - **ทำไม**: **เพราะเหตุใด** (_phrɔ́ hèet day_ – formal)
    - **ทาน**: **กิน** (_kin_ – casual), **รับประทาน** (_ráp-pra-thaan_ – formal)
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **ทำไมเด็กๆ ถึงชอบทานอาหารตอนดึกครับ** (_tham-may dèk-dèk thǓŋ chɔ̂ɔp thaan ?aa-hăan toon-dUuk khráp_)

### Item 13: โรงเรียน ไป ทาง ไหน

1. **Literal Translation**: School + go + direction/way + which.
2. **Meaning**: "Which way is the school?" / "How do I get to the school?"
3. **Word Breakdown**:
    - **โรงเรียน** (_rooŋ-rian_)
    - **ไป** (_pay_)
    - **ทาง** (_thaaŋ_)
    - **ไหน** (_náy_)
4. **Context & English Meaning**:
    - **โรงเรียน** (_rooŋ-rian_): School (compound of **โรง** _facility_ + **เรียน** _study_).
    - **ไป** (_pay_): Go (uses **สระไอ ไ-**).
    - **ทาง** (_thaaŋ_): Direction, route, or path.
    - **ไหน** (_náy_): Which / Where (uses **สระไอ ไ-**).
5. **Modern Daily Usage**: Essential street phrase for asking directions from locals or drivers.
6. **Formality**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **ขอโทษครับ โรงเรียนไปทางไหนครับ** (_khɔ̌ɔ-thôot khráp, rooŋ-rian pay thaaŋ náy khráp_) – Excuse me, which way is the school?
8. **Synonyms**:
    - **ทางไหน**: **ทางใด** (_thaaŋ-day_ – formal)
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **ขอโทษครับ โรงเรียนไปทางไหนครับ** (_khɔ̌ɔ-thôot khráp, rooŋ-rian pay thaaŋ náy khráp_)

### Item 14: นักเรียน ดีใจ เพราะ อะไร

1. **Literal Translation**: Student(s) + happy/glad + because + what.
2. **Meaning**: "Why are the students happy?" / "The students are happy because of what?"
3. **Word Breakdown**:

    - **ดีใจ** (_dii-cay_)
    - **เพราะ** (_phrɔ́_)

4. **Context & English Meaning**:
    - **นักเรียน** (_nák-rian_): Student(s).

6. **Formality**: Neutral.

### Key Target Vocabulary Breakdown

#### 1. **เมื่อวานนี้** (_mUa-waan-níi_)

1. **Literal Translation**: When + past day + this.
2. **Meaning**: Yesterday (the day before today).
3. **Word Breakdown**:
    - **เมื่อ** (_mUa_) – When / At the time of.
    - **วาน** (_waan_) – Yesterday / Past day.
    - **นี้** (_níi_) – This.
4. **Cultural / Historical Context**: **วาน** originates from Old Thai referring to the cycle of time that has passed.
5. **Modern Daily Usage**: Placed at the beginning or end of a sentence to establish that an event happened yesterday.
6. **Formality**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **เมื่อวานนี้ผมไปเรียนภาษาไทยครับ** (_mUa-waan-níi phŏm pay rian phaa-săa thay khráp_) – Yesterday I went to study Thai.
8. **Synonyms**: **เมื่อวาน** (_mUa-waan_ – casual short form), **วานนี้** (_waan-níi_ – formal/written).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **เมื่อวานนี้ผมไปอ่านหนังสือที่ห้องสมุดครับ** (_mUa-waan-níi phŏm pay ?àan naŋsUu thîi hông samùt khráp_) – Yesterday I went to read books at the library.

---

#### 2. **วันนี้** (_wan-níi_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Day + this.
2. **Meaning**: Today (the current day).
3. **Word Breakdown**:
    - **วัน** (_wan_) – Day.
    - **นี้** (_níi_) – This.
4. **Cultural / Historical Context**: Standard spatial-temporal marker combining **วัน** (_day_) with the demonstrative **นี้** (_this_).
5. **Modern Daily Usage**: Used constantly when discussing current plans, states, or schedules.
6. **Formality**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **วันนี้ผมมีเรียนตอนเช้าครับ** (_wan-níi phŏm mii rian toon cháaw khráp_) – Today I have class in the morning.
8. **Synonyms**: **ในวันนี้** (_nay wan-níi_ – formal/written).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **วันนี้ผมว่างตอนบ่ายครับ** (_wan-níi phŏm wâaŋ toon bàay khráp_) – Today I am free in the afternoon.

---

#### 3. **พรุ่งนี้** (_phrûŋ-níi_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Dawn/Tomorrow + this.
2. **Meaning**: Tomorrow (the day after today).
3. **Word Breakdown**:
    - **พรุ่ง** (_phrûŋ_) – Tomorrow / Dawn.
    - **นี้** (_níi_) – This.
4. **Cultural / Historical Context**: **พรุ่ง** historically connects to the break of dawn of the upcoming day.
5. **Modern Daily Usage**: Essential daily word when arranging upcoming appointments or future plans.
6. **Formality**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **พรุ่งนี้ผมจะไปทานข้าวกับเพื่อนครับ** (_phrûŋ-níi phŏm ca pay thaan khâaw kàp phûan khráp_) – Tomorrow I will go eat with my friend.
8. **Synonyms**: **วันพรุ่งนี้** (_wan-phrûŋ-níi_ – clear/formal).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **พรุ่งนี้เราเจอกันตอนเที่ยงนะครับ** (_phrûŋ-níi raw cəə kan toon thîaŋ ná khráp_) – Tomorrow let's meet at noon, okay?

---

#### 4. **วันจันทร์** (_wan-can_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Day + Moon.
2. **Meaning**: Monday.
3. **Word Breakdown**:
    - **วัน** (_wan_) – Day.
    - **จันทร์** (_can_) – Moon (derived from Sanskrit _Chandra_).
4. **Cultural / Historical Context**: In Thai culture, each day of the week is named after a celestial body and associated with a traditional color. Monday (**วันจันทร์**) represents the Moon and its traditional color is yellow (**สีเหลือง**).
5. **Modern Daily Usage**: Used daily when making weekly schedules or discussing work and school days.
6. **Formality**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **วันจันทร์ผมมีเรียนแปดโมงเช้าครับ** (_wan-can phŏm mii rian pɛ̀ɛt mooŋ cháaw khráp_) – On Monday I have class at 8:00 AM.
8. **Synonyms**: N/A (specific day of the week).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **ทุกวันจันทร์ผมไปเรียนภาษาไทยครับ** (_thúk wan-can phŏm pay rian phaa-săa thay khráp_) – Every Monday I go study Thai.

### Key Target Vocabulary Breakdown

#### 1. **คิดเงินด้วย** (_khít ŋen dûay_)

7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **ผมอยากสั่งผัดไทยกุ้งครับ** (_phŏm yàak sàŋ phàt thay kúŋ khráp_) – I would like to order shrimp Pad Thai.
8. **Synonyms**: **สั่งซื้อ** (_sàŋ-sÚu_ – order to buy), **รับ** (_ráp_ – take/order).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **ขอสั่งอาหารหน่อยครับ** (_khɔ̌ɔ sàŋ ?aa-hăan nɔ̀oy khráp_) – May I order food, please?

#### 2. **เยอะ** (_yə́_)

1. **Literal Translation**: A lot / Numerous / Plentiful.
2. **Meaning**: Expresses a large quantity or abundance of items, people, or actions.
3. **Word Breakdown**: **เยอะ** (_yə́_) – High tone.
4. **Cultural & Contextual Usage**: Common conversational alternative to **มาก** (_mâak_). Frequently used to describe crowds or abundant menu choices.
5. **Modern Daily Usage**: Extremely common in daily spoken Thai when describing high numbers of people, tasks, or food portions.
6. **Formality**: Casual to Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **วันนี้มีคนมาทานอาหารเยอะมากครับ** (_wan-níi mii khon maa thaan ?aa-hăan yə́ mâak khráp_) – Today a lot of people came to eat.
8. **Synonyms**: **มาก** (_mâak_ – very/much), **เยอะแยะ** (_yə́-yɛ́_ – casual plentiful).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **ที่ร้านนี้คนเยอะมากครับ** (_thîi ráan níi khon yə́ mâak khráp_) – At this restaurant, there are a lot of people.

#### 3. **แก้ว** (_kɛ̂ɛw_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Glass / Tumbler.
2. **Meaning**: A drinking glass container; also serves as the classifier for drinks served in glasses or cups.
3. **Word Breakdown**: **แก้ว** (_kɛ̂ɛw_) – Falling tone.
4. **Cultural & Contextual Usage**: Standard unit classifier used when ordering beverages (e.g., iced tea, water, lime juice) at restaurants and food stalls.
5. **Modern Usage**: Used daily when specifying the number of drinks ordered.
6. **Formality**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **ขอน้ำเปล่าสองแก้วครับ** (_khɔ̌ɔ náam plàaw sɔ̌ɔŋ kɛ̂ɛw khráp_) – May I have two glasses of plain water, please.
8. **Synonyms**: **ถ้วย** (_thûay_ – cup/bowl for hot drinks or soups).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **ขอน้ำมะนาวแก้วหนึ่งครับ** (_khɔ̌ɔ náam ma-naaw kɛ̂ɛw nUuŋ khráp_) – May I have one glass of lime juice, please.

#### 4. **เพราะ** (_phrɔ́_)

1. **Literal Translation**: Because / Due to.
2. **Meaning**: Conjunction introducing a reason or cause ("because").
3. **Word Breakdown**: **เพราะ** (_phrɔ́_) – High tone.
4. **Cultural & Contextual Usage**: Essential logical connector used when explaining motives, reasons, or answers to "why" (**ทำไม** _tham-may_) questions.
5. **Modern Daily Usage**: Used constantly in everyday speech when giving explanations.
6. **Formality**: Neutral.
7. **Daily Example Sentence**: **ผมชอบทานผัดไทยเพราะอร่อยมากครับ** (_phŏm chɔ̂ɔp thaan phàt thay phrɔ́ ?aròy mâak khráp_) – I like eating Pad Thai because it is very delicious.
8. **Synonyms**: **เพราะว่า** (_phrɔ́-wâa_ – because that), **เนื่องจาก** (_nUaŋ-càak_ – formal owing to).
9. **Thai Conversational Version**: **ผมไปทานข้าวที่นั่นเพราะอาหารอร่อยครับ** (_phŏm pay thaan khâaw thîi nân phrɔ́ ?aa-hăan ?aròy khráp_) – I go eat there because the food is delicious.
