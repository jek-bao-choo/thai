งาน วันเกิด = birthday function

In Thai, **"to bring"** depends on whether you are bringing an object or a person:

- **เอามา** (_ao-maa_) – To bring (an inanimate object).
    
      
    - Literally: "take + come"
        
          
        
    - _Example:_ เอาหนังสือมา (_ao nǎng-sǔue maa_) = "Bring the book."
        
          
        
- **พามา** (_phaa-maa_) – To bring (a person or pet along).
    
      
    - Literally: "guide/lead + come"
        
          
        
    - _Example:_ พาเพื่อนมา (_phaa phûean maa_) = "Bring a friend."
        
          
        
- **นำมา** (_nam-maa_) – To bring / introduce (formal or written Thai).
    
      
    - _Example:_ นำข้อมูลมา (_nam khâaw-moon maa_) = "To bring data/information."
        
          
        

_(Note: If you mean to **take** something away rather than bring it here, swap มา / _maa_ with ไป / _bpai_, e.g., เอาไป / _ao-bpai_)._


**ใคร** (_khrai_) = Who


In Thai, the word order splits **พา** (_to bring / lead_) and **มา** (_come_), placing the person being brought in the middle:

  

$$\text{พา } + [\text{person brought}] + \text{ มา}$$

Because **ใคร** (_khrai_) can act as the subject ("who"), the direct object ("whom"), or an indefinite pronoun ("anyone"), here are the standard sentence patterns:

  

### 1. "Who brought [someone] here?"

- **ใคร** acts as the subject:
    
      
    

> **ใครพาเขามา?**
> 
>   
> 
> _khrai phaa khǎo maa?_
> 
>   
> 
> "Who brought him/her here?"
> 
>   

> **ใครพาคุณครูมา?**
> 
>   
> 
> _khrai phaa khun-khruu maa?_
> 
>   
> 
> "Who brought the teacher here?"
> 
>   

### 2. "Whom did you bring?"

- **ใคร** goes between **พา** and **มา** as the object:
    
      
    

> **คุณพาใครมา?**
> 
>   
> 
> _khun phaa khrai maa?_
> 
>   
> 
> "Whom did you bring along?"
> 
>   

### 3. "Don't bring anyone" / "Can I bring someone?"

- **ใคร** functions as "anyone" or "someone":
    
      
    

> **อย่าพาใครมานะ**
> 
>   
> 
> _yàa phaa khrai maa ná_
> 
>   
> 
> "Don't bring anyone, alright?"
> 
>   

> **พาใครมาก็ได้**
> 
>   
> 
> _phaa khrai maa gâaw-dâai_
> 
>   
> 
> "You can bring whoever / anyone you like."
