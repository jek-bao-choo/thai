**ผมเห็นมือถือใหม่** (I saw a new phone) + **ที่ร้าน iPhone** (at the iPhone store) + **ทั้งสวยและดูดี** (both beautiful and looks good) + **แต่ผมคิดว่า** (but I think that) + **ผมยังไม่ซื้อดีกว่า** (it’s better if I don't buy it yet) + **เพราะราคาแพงไป** (because the price is too expensive).

I saw a new phone at the iPhone store. It's both beautiful and looks great, but I think it's better if I don't buy it yet because the price is too high.

#### **มือถือ (Meu-tĕu)**

- **Phonetic:** _meu-tĕu_ (Mid tone - Rising tone)
    
- **Meaning:** Mobile phone (Literal: "Hand-carry").
    
- **Usage:** The most common way to say "phone" today.
    
- **Register:** Neutral/Casual.
    
- **Example:** **มือถือ**หาย (_meu-tĕu hăai_) — The **phone** is lost.
    
- **Synonyms:** **โทรศัพท์** (_toh-ra-sàp_ - formal/telephone).

## เลย

**Pronunciation:** loei  
**Meaning:** so / therefore / as a result / immediately (depending on context)

### Usage:

Very common conversational connector.

In this sentence:  
→ expresses consequence  
“So…” / “That’s why…”

### Tone Level:

Casual to neutral. Extremely common in speech.

### Example:

ฝนตกเลยไม่ได้ไป  
fǒn tòk loei mâi dâai bpai  
“It rained, so I couldn’t go.”

### Synonyms:

- ก็เลย (gôr loei) → very common spoken form


**ดินทาง** means:

- to travel
    
- to journey
    
- to go somewhere (especially longer distance)
    

Even though เดิน = walk, the word does **NOT** mean “walk travel.”  
It means **travel in general** (car, plane, bus, walking — anything).

## ทาง

**Pronunciation:** taang  
**Meaning:** way / path / direction / route

### Usage:

- Physical road
    
- Direction
    
- Method (in some contexts)
    

### Tone Level:

Neutral.

### Example:

ทางนี้ครับ  
taang née kráp  
“This way.”

### Synonyms:

- เส้นทาง (sên taang) → route (more specific)


**เมืองนอก** means:

- foreign country
    
- overseas
    
- abroad
    

It literally means “outside the country,” but in real usage it means **another country**, especially Western countries.

## เมือง

**Pronunciation:** mueang  
**Meaning:** city / town / country (depending on context)

### Usage:

- เมืองไทย = Thailand
    
- เมืองจีน = China
    
- ในเมือง = in town
    

Thai uses เมือง flexibly — sometimes it means “country,” not just city.

### Tone Level:

Neutral and very common.

### Example:

ผมอยู่ในเมือง  
“I live in town.”

## นอก

**Pronunciation:** nôrk  
**Meaning:** outside / external

### Usage:

- ข้างนอก = outside
    
- นอกบ้าน = outside the house
    

### Tone Level:

Neutral.

### Example:

เขาอยู่นอกบ้าน  
“He is outside the house.”

### Synonyms:

- ภายนอก (paa-nâai-nôrk) → exterior (formal)
    
- ข้างนอก (kâang nôrk) → outside (very common)

เมืองนอก = foreign country / overseas

⚠️ Cultural nuance:

Thai people often use เมืองนอก to refer to:

- Western countries
    
- Europe
    
- America
    
- Australia
    

Sometimes it implies something “international” or “foreign-style.”

Example:  
ของจากเมืองนอก  
“Products from overseas”

## กลาง

**Pronunciation:** /glaang/ (mid tone)

### Meaning:

- middle
    
- center
    
- central
    

### Usage:

Used to describe something in the middle of space or time.

Examples:

- กลางคืน (middle of the night = nighttime)
    
- กลางถนน (middle of the road)
    

### Register:

Neutral. Very common in daily Thai.

### Example:

- เขายืนอยู่กลางถนน  
    (khăo yeun yùu glaang thanŏn)  
    → He is standing in the middle of the road.
